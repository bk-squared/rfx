### Fixed — graded multi-device run() above 5:1 grading falls back like forward() (#1918)

- `run(devices=...)` on a graded mesh steeper than 5:1 with a waveguide port or TF/SF source
  now runs on one device with the fallback warning, as `forward(distributed=True)` does,
  instead of raising the multi-device grading-ratio `ValueError`.

### Fixed — run(until_decay=) stops on an open plane wave with the default pulse (#1918)

The default TF/SF pulse now shares GaussianPulse's continuous, charge-free start.
An empty open plane-wave box can stop after the pulse leaves instead of running to the step cap.
The maximum pulse adjustment is 1.6e-3 of its amplitude (rounded to two significant figures).
