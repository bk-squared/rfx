### Fixed — a PEC or PMC wall beside the absorber is honoured on a mesh split across devices (#1235)

- On `run(devices=N)` (uniform mesh) the absorber was built on all six faces whatever the boundary declared. A PEC or PMC wall,
  such as a ground plane or a half-model symmetry plane, then had an absorber in front of it, and the field next to the wall
  differed from the one-device run by up to 1.6 times its peak. On `forward(distributed=True)` (non-uniform mesh) a PMC wall had the
  same defect (1.0 times its peak). Both multi-device paths now take every face's absorber from the one-device builder, and each
  PEC or PMC face agrees with one device to within 1e-5 of the peak.
- The uniform multi-device path also builds the requested per-face absorber depth. The non-uniform lane still builds one depth on
  every face (#1346).
