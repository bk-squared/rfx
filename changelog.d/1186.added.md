### Added — a record-length witness for gradients, the companion to the −40 dB settling witness for values (#1186, #1181)

- `gradient_record_length_witness(objective, params, n_steps, *, tol, factor=2.0)`
  differentiates the caller's objective at `n_steps` and again at
  `ceil(factor * n_steps)` and reports, per frequency bin,
  `‖g_long − g_short‖ / ‖g_long‖` over the whole parameter vector plus the
  cosine between the two arms, with the two records' settling levels beside
  them. A resonance still ringing at the end of the record leaves a term in
  every DFT bin whose phase is `(ω − ω_r)·T`; a parameter that moves the
  resonance spins that phase, and the spin enters the derivative magnified by
  the record length while the value hides it. Measured on a grounded-slab
  patch, a 2200-step record that passes the −40 dB settling rule gives |S11|²
  within 0.99 % and `d ln|S11|²/d ln εr` out by 9.7 … 23 %.
- Complex observables — an S-parameter, a DFT phasor — are compared as complex
  sensitivities (`dRe/dp + i dIm/dp`). A parameter that rotates a phasor at
  constant magnitude moves only the imaginary part, so half of the sensitivity
  is not a check. Parameters must be real; complex and integer leaves are
  refused with a message.
- The per-element table is reported for locating a failure, not for deciding
  one: an element carrying a millionth of the dominant sensitivity moves by
  100 % on its own rounding.
- `tol` is required and has no default: the bar depends on the structure's Q and
  on what the gradient is for. The docstring carries the measured sweep to
  calibrate against.
- Exported as `rfx.gradient_record_length_witness` /
  `rfx.GradientRecordLengthWitness`; `docs/guides/known_limitations.md` gains the
  matching entry.
