### BREAKING — the coaxial line lanes absorb on all three axes, so an open end is passive and every coaxial S moves slightly (#1218)

- `compute_coaxial_line_reflection` and `compute_coaxial_two_port` now hand the
  runner `cpml_axes="xyz"`, the new default and the only value accepted;
  `cpml_axes="z"` raises `ValueError`. With absorbers on z only, the lateral
  pads were vacuum backed by the PEC grid faces: a closed metal can around the
  line that held the field leaving its unshielded ends. The open termination
  then read |S11| above 1 and grew with the record (1.018 at 12 line
  traversals and 1.046 at 24, at 9 annulus cells); with the lateral pads
  absorbing it reads at most 1.0015 at both.
- Every face needs a positive CPML thickness: a zero-thickness face has no
  absorber and is a PEC wall. A line whose realized outer wall comes within one
  whole cell of a lateral absorber is refused rather than run.
- Migration: drop `cpml_axes="z"` from calls to either lane. Results computed
  before this change came from the closed can and do not reproduce.
