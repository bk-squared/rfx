### Fixed — Several GPUs in one process: preparing the slab material coefficients no longer holds repeated copies beside their whole-grid inputs, so on two GPUs the per-GPU peak falls below one GPU's (#2150)

- Before, `sim.run(devices=...)` copied a material array into each GPU's slab once for every place the model refers to it, and kept every input alive until all its copies were made. On two RTX A6000 GPUs, a 1024 x 64 x 192-cell box with a wire port peaked at 1.764 GB per GPU, above one GPU's 1.680 GB.
- Now each array is copied once per fill value (the value placed outside the grid), and its input is released once its copies exist. The whole-grid arrays are released right after they are cut into slabs.
- The same box now peaks at 0.832 GB per GPU on two GPUs, and the time loop sets that peak. Fields, probes and port records are bit-identical to before.
- GPU 0 still builds the whole-grid material arrays first (0.665 GB on this box); see the known limitations.
