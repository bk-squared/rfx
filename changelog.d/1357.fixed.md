### Fixed — a permittivity gradient stays finite at any objective scale (#1357)

- `jax.grad` with respect to `eps_override` returned NaN or ±inf at the cells that matter most once the objective was large. A sum of squared probe fields on a graded mesh is large enough: the #1357 fixture (about 2e17) had 16 non-finite cells. On the ADI lane (`solver="adi"`) every cell was non-finite at any scale.
- The uniform, graded-mesh and ADI lanes now return a finite gradient that scales exactly with the objective. This covers subpixel smoothing, CPML pads, resistive sheets and lumped elements. Forward results are bit-identical.
- Still affected: Debye/Lorentz materials and `forward(distributed=True)`. They follow the rewrite in #1325.
