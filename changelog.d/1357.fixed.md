### Fixed — a permittivity gradient stays finite at any objective scale (#1357)

- `jax.grad` with respect to `eps_override` returned NaN or ±inf at the cells that matter most
  once the objective was large. A sum of squared probe fields on a graded mesh is large enough:
  the #1357 fixture (about 2e17) had 16 non-finite cells. 2-D ADI (`solver="adi"`) was
  non-finite at every cell at any scale, and 3-D ADI from an objective of about 1.
- The uniform lane (CPML, UPML, PEC and PMC faces), the graded-mesh lane,
  `forward(distributed=True)` and both ADI lanes now return a finite gradient that scales
  exactly with the objective, with subpixel smoothing, resistive sheets and lumped elements
  too, including Debye, Lorentz and mixed Debye+Lorentz materials on the supported lanes.
  Forward results retain their SI arithmetic and are bit-identical.
