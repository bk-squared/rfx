### Changed — preflight stops a model whose drawn gap between two PEC sheets is solved as metal (#1609)

PEC sheets on one plane, declared by `add_thin_conductor` or zero-thickness PEC boxes
passed to `add()`, are joined when no free node line lies between them — always for a drawn
gap of one cell or less, and for a gap up to two cells depending on where it falls on the mesh.
Preflight reports `sheet_gap_bridged`, naming both declarations, the drawn gap and the number
of edges not already made metal by any single sheet. `run()` and `forward()` stop at the
preflight gate (`skip_preflight=True` runs as before). Touching or overlapping bounds are not
reported; non-box shapes are judged by their bounding boxes. The realization is unchanged;
the fix (join only where drawn extents touch or overlap) follows. Draw the gap at least two
cells wide or refine the mesh there. See `docs/guides/known_limitations.md`.
