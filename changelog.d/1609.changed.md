### Changed — preflight stops a model whose drawn gap between two PEC sheets is solved as metal (#1609)

Two `add_thin_conductor` PEC sheets on one plane drawn less than two cells apart (a feed gap,
a coupling gap, a slot) are solved as ONE conductor: the edges across the gap are metal.
Preflight now reports this as the error `sheet_gap_bridged`, naming both sheets, the drawn gap
and the number of bridged edges, so `run()` and `forward()` stop at the preflight gate
(`skip_preflight=True` runs the model as before). Sheets drawn touching or overlapping are
not reported. The realization itself is unchanged; the fix (join only where the drawn extents
touch) follows. Workaround: draw the gap at least two cells wide or refine the mesh there.
See `docs/guides/known_limitations.md`.
