`add_wall_loss` on a uniform mesh now holds the magnetic row half a cell outside every non-periodic high domain face at zero, as the graded mesh does. In a 30 x 12 x 24 mm cavity with 5.8e5 S/m walls at 8 GHz on 1 mm cells, Q rises by 2.4e-4 (x pair), 1.8e-4 (y) and 1.5e-4 (z). Lossless results are unchanged when CPML faces retain their PEC backing. Runs that withhold the PEC backing of a CPML face through the low-level `pec_axes` argument move: the measured field-hold prototype changed complex S by at most |dS| = 3.8e-7 on the T-junction, and the withheld S2 record by 8.5e-9 per step and 7.4e-7 in r (withholding itself: 2.0e-8 and 1.3e-6). The affected public functions are `rfx.simulation.run`, `rfx.simulation.run_until_decay`, `extract_waveguide_s_matrix`, `extract_waveguide_s_matrix_flux`, `extract_waveguide_s_params_normalized`, `extract_multimode_s_matrix`, and `extract_multimode_s_matrix_flux` (the five extractors in `rfx.measurement.waveguide_s`, also exported by `rfx.sources.waveguide_port`).
