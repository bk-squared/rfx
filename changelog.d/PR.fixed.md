`add_wall_loss` on a uniform mesh now holds the magnetic field row half a cell outside every non-periodic high domain face at zero, as the graded mesh does. Previously a lossy high face read that row and lost excess power. In a 30 × 12 × 24 mm copper cavity at 8 GHz on 1 mm cells, Q rises by 2.4e-4 for the x wall pair, 1.8e-4 for y, and 1.5e-4 for z (relative changes). Lossless results are unchanged.
