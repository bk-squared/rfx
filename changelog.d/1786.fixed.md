### Fixed — Formulation-aware AD memory plans (#1786)

AD memory planning now counts the tape for the selected design, checkpoint flag, and segments.
In the verified 49³ design-box example, the full-AD estimate falls from 11.30 GB to 0.583 GB (19.4×).
Segmented plans recommend checkpoint=True; segments without it retain the whole tape (rfx#1789).
Plans use a 1.15 margin on compiled program memory; allocator overhead uses the budget headroom.
