### Fixed — `add_wall_loss` on a uniform mesh lost slightly too much power on each lossy high domain face (#2615)

- The uniform mesh now holds the magnetic row half a cell outside every non-periodic high domain face at zero, as the graded
  mesh does. In a 30 x 12 x 24 mm cavity with 5.8e5 S/m walls at 8 GHz on 1 mm cells, Q rises by 2.4e-4 (x pair), 1.8e-4 (y)
  and 1.5e-4 (z) and now matches the graded mesh. Lossless results do not change where every CPML face keeps its PEC backing.
- Where `pec_axes` withholds a CPML face's PEC backing, S, port V/I and probe records of `run`, `run_until_decay`,
  `extract_waveguide_s_matrix`, `extract_waveguide_s_matrix_flux`, `extract_waveguide_s_params_normalized`,
  `extract_multimode_s_matrix` and `extract_multimode_s_matrix_flux` move by at most about 1e-6 relative.
- One exception: a low-level `WaveguidePort` given the whole node plane (`y_slice=(0, grid.ny)`) drives and reads the field
  row outside the high PEC wall, which is now held. Its higher-mode S entries move (11 % of the largest entry in a two-mode
  test) while the fields inside the guide move at most 3e-7 of their peak; those entries already differed from the
  cell-span port's by more than their own size. Ports built through `Simulation` stop one cell short and do not move.
