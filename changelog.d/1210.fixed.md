### Fixed — a Yee E component takes the mean of its four incident cells' ε and σ, so a volume conductor's plus faces sit where they were drawn (#1210)

- A conductor entered as a volume conductivity (`add_material(sigma=…)` below
  the 1e6 S/m PEC threshold, `sigma_override`, `design_sigma_override`) was
  realized one cell thin on its plus faces: `update_e` built one cell-centred
  `(Ca, Cb)` and multiplied Ex, Ey and Ez by it, so three of the four cells each
  E edge touches were ignored. A 6 mm σ = 1e5 S/m slab on the floor of a 24 mm
  PEC cube read its half-wave-along-z mode at 10.0585 GHz — the answer for a
  5 mm slab — against 10.4051 GHz through the PEC occupancy lane and 10.4095 GHz
  analytic, 3.3 % low. With the edge average the same fixture reads 10.4051 GHz,
  the PEC lane's number to 2.3e-9 relative.
- Dielectric interfaces carried the same rule at lower order: the tangential E
  on a dielectric's plus face took the air cell's ε (first-order) instead of the
  mean (second-order).
- `rfx.core.yee.edge_averaged_materials` is the one spelling of the rule — per
  component, the arithmetic mean of ε_r and σ over the four cells incident to
  that component's edge (parallel conductances for σ, the Kottke/Taflove §10
  tangential entry for ε). Periodic axes wrap exactly as
  `rfx.boundaries.pec._shift` does; non-periodic axes edge-replicate, because a
  permittivity of zero outside the lattice is a division by zero. Homogeneous
  regions are bit-identical: `((a + a) + (a + a)) * 0.25 == a` in float32.
- Converted: `update_e`, `update_e_nu` (graded mesh), `precompute_coeffs` (the
  GPU coefficient bake, new `periodic=` argument), `update_e_box` (the #1179
  design box — `Ca`/`Cb` may now be per-component 3-tuples, and its write window
  grows one cell on the plus side of each axis so the box-edge cells average
  with the constant background), the CPML ψ permittivity on both plain lanes
  (`inv_eps_r_update` is now threaded whenever the E update is non-dispersive,
  not only on the subpixel paths — #1043's consistency requirement), and the
  whole-grid `eps_override` / `sigma_override` paths, which reach it through
  `update_e`. `vmap_sweep` and the subgridding runners call `update_e` and are
  covered with it; `vmap_sweep` additionally now threads the per-component
  permittivity into its own `apply_cpml_e`, which it had never done (#1043's
  requirement) and which is what kept it reproducing `run()`.
- A dielectric interface is now second-order instead of first. Measured on a
  24 mm PEC cube half-filled with ε_r = 4 (closed form from the transverse
  resonance, 7.2014 GHz): the cell-owned rule read 7.4606 GHz at dx = 1 mm
  (3.6 % high) and 7.3323 GHz at dx = 0.5 mm (1.8 %), halving with the mesh;
  the edge average reads 7.1918 GHz (0.13 %) and 7.1990 GHz (0.033 %),
  quartering with the mesh.
- A lumped element is NOT a cell material and is kept out of the average. A
  port's load, an RLC resistor or capacitor is folded into its cell's σ / ε_r
  as an equivalent for a device across ONE edge; averaged, a 50 Ω wire port
  read |S11| = 0.20 against the closed-form 1/3 and a lumped port in a CPML
  dielectric read a non-physical |S11| = 1.86. `MaterialArrays` gained
  `sigma_lumped` / `eps_r_lumped`, which record the stamp so
  `component_e_materials` can remove it before averaging and add it back at
  its own cell. All stamping — lumped port, wire port, MSL port termination,
  coaxial port gap, RLC R and C on both the concrete and the traced path —
  goes through `stamp_lumped_sigma` / `stamp_lumped_eps`. Both fields default
  to `None`, which is bit-identical to averaging `eps_r` and `sigma` outright.
- The design box's three fences (CPML absorber, source/port cells, loaded sheet
  edges) are now held against that one-cell-wider write window. A box that ended
  flush against the absorber, a source, or a sheet is refused where it was
  accepted before.
- NOT converted, deliberately: the **distributed lane** keeps its own inline
  copy of the coefficient and stays cell-owned — its slab material arrays carry
  the x halo, but `split_array_x` fills the halo at the physical x faces with
  vacuum rather than replicating the boundary cell, so averaging locally would
  agree with the single-device lane everywhere except those two faces.
  `tests/unit/runners/test_distributed_e_coefficients_are_still_cell_owned.py`
  documents the difference and goes red when the lane is converted. The **ADI**
  integrator builds its own semi-implicit coefficients (`σ·dt/4`, `eps_plus`),
  a different derivation, and is likewise untouched. The Kottke `aniso_inv_eps`
  / `aniso_eps` lanes were already per-component in ε and keep a cell-owned σ.
  Dispersive (Debye/Lorentz) updates, UPML, the #677 sheet operator, lumped
  elements and source-cell drive coefficients are per-cell or per-edge
  coefficients of their own and are out of this change.
- Locks that moved, with the interface that moved each: one,
  `tests/locks/test_sheet_refactor_bit_identity.py::test_update_kernels_bit_identity_via_curl_helpers`,
  moved by up to 1.6× relative on its RANDOM-material fixture. The interface
  that moved it is the material-to-edge rule itself: the lock's hand-written
  reference built one cell-centred `(Ca, Cb)` and applied it to all three
  components. Re-pinned to the per-component reference; the coefficient formula
  it exists to watch (`e_update_coeffs`) is unchanged. Every other lock in
  `tests/locks` is green unchanged, which is what bit-identity on a homogeneous
  fixture predicts.
