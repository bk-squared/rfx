### Changed — localized dispersive materials use less memory and time (#9999, #1825)

- Debye, Lorentz, Drude and mixed materials keep their polarization state only
  on the box around the material's electric edges (uniform and graded meshes,
  multi-device runs included), instead of on the whole domain.
- Added cost per time step for a 32×16×16-cell block in a 160³ grid (CPU):
  Lorentz/Drude about 21 ms → 1 ms, Debye 34 ms → 13 ms.
- Results within float32 rounding of the previous version (not bit-identical;
  largest difference 0.7 of the accumulated-rounding bound).
