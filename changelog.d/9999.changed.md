### Multi-device UPML and ADI refusals come from one check (#9999)

- `boundary='upml'` or `solver='adi'` with more than one device is refused by one
  check that `run()` and `forward()` both call. On a graded mesh, `forward(distributed=True)`
  with UPML now raises the same `ValueError` text as `run()` ("does not support
  distributed execution") instead of its graded-lane text. On a graded mesh,
  `forward(distributed=True)` with `solver='adi'` now refuses with that text instead of
  "requires a uniform mesh". No admitted run changes.
