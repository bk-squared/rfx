### Changed — `run(ringdown=...)`: the completed S-matrix is checked against a completion from a window of double span, so some short records that read ok before now read not ok (#1254)

- The report's error witness is now `WE`: the completion from the main
  window [T/2, T] against the completion from the window of double span
  [T/4, T], the largest |S| difference over every bin and entry, bar
  `witness_tol` (1e-3). The previous witness `W2` ([T/2, T] against
  [T/2, 0.9 T]) is still computed and reported, but no longer decides
  `report.ok` (`RingdownWitness.judged` is False for it; `report.ok` reads
  the judged witnesses only). On the research boards W2 read the actual
  error 0.003-22x and under half of it on 6 of 19 records; WE read it
  0.84-3.2x on 18 of the 19 (the one miss: two modes 0.03 % apart that no
  window of the record separates).
- When a source is still on in [T/4, T], WE is not formed: the report
  lists it as not formed and judges W2 in its place, with a note saying so.
- The completed S itself (`Result.ringdown.s_params`) is unchanged, bit for
  bit, and so is every other output.
- Some records that read ok before now read not ok. On the 12 × 11 × 5 mm
  metal box graded 0.6-1.4 mm on all axes (TM110 at 12.40 GHz), a 1.72 ns
  record (1500 steps) completes S11 to 1.65e-3 of the completed 30,000-step
  answer: WE reads 1.6e-3 and fails it; W2 read 3.0e-4 and passed it under
  JAX 0.10.2 (1.7e-3 under JAX 0.4.33). At 2.86 ns (2500 steps) the error is
  8.9e-5 and WE reads 9.8e-5.
