### Fixed — the WR-90 dielectric-taper example's SMOKE lane now solves WR-90 (#1100)

- `validation/tmtt_paper/waveguide_dielectric_taper.py` declared 22.86 × 10.16 mm
  and meshed at dx = 1.0 mm, which neither wall divides, so the lane solved a
  23.0 × 11.0 mm guide. It now meshes at dx = 1.27 mm — gcd(22.86, 10.16) is
  2.54 mm — giving 18 × 8 transverse cells and a realized aperture equal to the
  declared one. `build_sim` asserts that, and preflight emits no
  `port_aperture_snap` row for the lane. The coarser mesh also cuts the SMOKE
  wall time from 132.6 s to 81.9 s.
- The paper lane keeps its 0.5 mm mesh, which realizes 23.0 × 10.5 mm. It is
  disclosed rather than re-meshed: `F_CUTOFF_TE10` and every other analytic
  reference now come from the realized walls, and the docstring says which guide
  each lane solves. Re-measuring the published figures on a commensurate mesh is
  a GPU-scale re-run and is not done here; #1122 carries that decision, and
  `docs/guides/known_limitations.md` keeps an entry against it.
- The `rfx/preflight/waveguide.py` docstrings that cite that lane's dx = 1 mm
  measurement now say it was the then-committed mesh, so the citation cannot be
  read as reproducible from today's script.
