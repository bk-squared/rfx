### Fixed — A traced input to compute_mixed_s_matrix gets a refusal that names it, instead of a raw JAX error (#2923)

`compute_mixed_s_matrix` is not differentiable. Under `jax.grad`, `jax.jvp`, `jax.jit` or `jax.vmap`, a traced value in its call arguments or in the simulation's declarations used to stop inside the calculator with a raw `ConcretizationTypeError` or `TracerArrayConversionError`. It now raises `NonDifferentiableEntry`, an `RfxRefusal`, at the call. The message names the traced input (for example `material 'sub' sigma`) and points to the differentiable routes: `compute_msl_s_matrix(eps_override=...)` for a microstrip-only model, and `forward(port_s11_freqs=...)` for lumped ports. Concrete calls are unchanged.
