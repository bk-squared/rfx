### Fixed — Consistent 2-D geometry cross-sections (#0000)

Two-dimensional models now use each volume's cross-section at the invariant z node,
with smoothing confined to the plane. An εr 4 box now retains εr 4 in its interior
when a declared z face lies within half a cell of that node; smoothing previously
could read 1.08–3.4. A volume wholly off the slice is refused by name, with guidance
to move its z extent onto the node. Faces exactly on the node count as present.
