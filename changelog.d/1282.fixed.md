### Fixed — `add_refinement` is refused where it used to be ignored (#1240, #1282)

- `add_refinement` on a non-uniform mesh (an axis profile, or one that auto-meshing
  produced) is now refused with a message. `preflight()` reports it as an error, and
  `run()`, `forward()` and `compute_waveguide_s_matrix` raise. Before, it was ignored
  without a word and the result was that of the unrefined mesh.
- On a uniform mesh, `forward()`, `optimize()`, `topology_optimize()`,
  `vmap_material_sweep()`, `compute_waveguide_s_matrix()` and
  `differentiable_material_fit()` now refuse it too. They have no subgridded lane and
  ignored it the same way. A refinement takes effect in `run()` on a uniform mesh, as
  before.
