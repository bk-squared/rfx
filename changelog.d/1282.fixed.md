### Fixed — a subgrid refinement on a non-uniform mesh was ignored without a word; it is now refused (#1240, #1282)

- `add_refinement(z_range, ratio)` on a non-uniform (graded) mesh did nothing. The
  graded-mesh lane has no subgrid and never read the refinement, so it solved the unrefined
  mesh. On a 12 mm PEC box with 1 mm cells, the probe was bit-identical to the same box
  without the refinement (peak 1.079170e6), with no warning, and `preflight()` passed. The
  same refinement on a uniform mesh moves the peak to 6.611695e4. This held for `run()`,
  `forward()`, `forward(distributed=True)`, the graded branch of
  `compute_waveguide_s_matrix`, and meshes that auto-meshing made non-uniform.
- `preflight()` now reports this as an error (code `nonuniform_refinement`), and `run()`
  and `forward()` raise, including with `skip_preflight=True`. To refine, keep
  `add_refinement` on a uniform base mesh (`dx=` with no axis profile), or remove it and
  pass a `dz_profile` whose cells are fine inside that z slab.
- Results on a uniform mesh, and on graded meshes without a refinement, do not change.
