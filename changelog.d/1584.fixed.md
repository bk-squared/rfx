### Microstrip port admission (#1584, #1433)

- A microstrip port declared before its substrate now gets the same automatic probe planes as one declared after it, on uniform and graded meshes. The automatic offset and spacing are taken from the whole declaration when the port is used, so a dielectric added later under the port now counts.
- A port extending sideways past its conductor reports the width axis and overhang instead of a height mismatch.
- Microstrip preflight reports geometry assembly refusals; unrelated failures such as MemoryError propagate to the caller.
