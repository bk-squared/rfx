### Declared conductor thickness advisory (#1947)

Preflight warns when a metal sheet's declared thickness exceeds 2 % of the nearest
overlapping Box dielectric's height and the sheet has an edge over that dielectric
(a strip or patch, not a ground plane covering it), and names the expected
electrical-length shift measured in #1523 (about +0.45 % at t/h 4.5 %, +0.99 % at 17.5 %).
