### Fixed — ADI in 2-D refuses a gradient with respect to permittivity or conductivity instead of returning NaN (#1450)

`jax.grad` through `forward(eps_override=...)` or `sigma_override` with
`solver='adi'` in 2-D returned NaN. It now raises before the first step and
names the alternatives (`solver='yee'`, or ADI in 3-D). The value, also under
`jax.jit`, is unchanged.
