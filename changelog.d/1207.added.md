### Added — a 50 ohm wire port runs on a mesh that is a JAX tracer, so `jax.grad` of S11 reaches a patch edge (#1207)

- Two of the wire port's quantities are cell sizes at the port's own cells:
  the termination conductance that makes the cell a 50 ohm load, and the
  metrics the recorded V and I are weighted by (the E edge's own length, and
  the dual spacings of the Ampere loop, #672). Both were host reads, so a
  `dx_profile` that was a tracer raised `TracerArrayConversionError` before a
  step ran, and the resonance from S11 — the observable an antenna engineer
  optimizes — was out of reach of the node-pinned conductor added in #1199.
  They now follow the mesh when it is traced, through the one helper
  `rfx.nonuniform.port_metric_axes`. Concrete meshes take the same float64
  host path as before, bit for bit.
- The per-cell source table stays traced too (it was already built in `jnp`);
  its overall scale cancels in the S11 ratio, so it carries no term of this
  derivative, and it is not routed through the helper.
- The single-cell lumped port and the extractor's per-port record
  (`_build_wp_meta`) follow the same rule, so the termination and the V/I
  measurement stay on one set of metrics.
- `Simulation(dt=..., dt_min_cell=...)` (and `make_nonuniform_grid` /
  `build_nonuniform_grid`) pin one concrete time step across a deformation
  family. The derived Courant step follows the smallest cell, so an unpinned
  sweep changes its step as the mesh deforms and the resonance carries the
  step change as well as the geometry change. The pin is refused, never
  clipped, when it exceeds the Courant limit of the realized cells;
  `dt_min_cell` is the declared floor for a traced axis, checked against the
  cells the run actually builds.
- A coordinate that names one node on the nominal mesh and another on the
  mesh a traced run builds is now refused by name instead of silently placing
  a source, probe or port somewhere else than the concrete run does.
