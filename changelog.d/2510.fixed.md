### Fixed — record every completed waveguide-port step (#2510)

An N-step run's waveguide-port V/I record now holds N samples, with no
unwritten zero last sample. S extraction sums the one added sample: unchanged
on a settled record. On a record cut before settling, the change grows as the
record gets shorter: about 5e-4 of max on a WR-90 record, and 4.5e-3 in the
largest |S| (0.1444 to 0.1489) of the 200-step S11 design example
(`examples/inverse_design/differentiable_s11_design.py`), whose objective
rose 6 %.
