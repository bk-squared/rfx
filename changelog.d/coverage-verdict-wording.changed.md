### Changed — The record-end coverage verdict names the step it computes, the drive's end plus a light-speed transit (#PR)

- An uncovered monitor's verdict (in `result.settling_witness["coverage"]`, its `reason`, and the `AccuracyWarning` that `run()` and `compute_rcs` give) now reads `cannot judge: the record ends at step N, before the drive has passed <monitor> (step M: the drive's end plus a light-speed transit)`. It said "before the field leaves <monitor>", but step M is a geometric bound: it counts neither repeated reflections nor slower travel in a material, so it does not show that the response has died away. Which monitors are covered, and every step number, are unchanged; only the text changed. Code that matched the old text has to match the new one.
- `examples/tutorials/rcs_scattering.py` and `slab_rt_flux_monitor.py` print and raise with the same wording. The slab tutorial now runs in `tests/contracts/test_tutorial_examples.py`, which asserts that both of its runs pass the ring-down witness.
- `docs/agent/recipe-rt-measurement.mdx`: the template uses one fixed record (`n_steps`) checked by the witness, as the tutorial does. With `run(until_decay=1e-5)` the vacuum reference run ended on the float32 floor with an `AccuracyWarning` in both measured cases (25,900 steps on the tutorial's slab, against its 1,200-step record), and `n_steps` is ignored; R and T came out the same as the tutorial's to three decimals, with the template's 20 CPML layers against the tutorial's 10.
