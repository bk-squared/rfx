### Fixed — Distributed run capability reporting

Graded multi-device `run(devices=...)` now has its own capability lane,
sharing the supported inputs of the nonuniform distributed runner with
`forward(distributed=True)`. Uniform-only ports, NTFF and CPML kappa are
no longer listed as supported by graded multi-device runs.
A 2-D model passed to multi-device `run(devices=...)` is refused with a
feature-naming message before stepping instead of crashing in CPML.
