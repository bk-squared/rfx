### Fixed — `SnapshotSpec(interval=m)` records every m-th step, and every recorded sample has a public position and time (#1258, #1259)

- A run with `snapshot=SnapshotSpec(interval=m, ...)` used to record every time
  step whatever `m` said, so memory grew with `n_steps` and a reader who took
  frame `k` as step `m*k` put every frame at the wrong time. It now records
  `n_steps // m` frames, frame `k` holding the fields after step `(k + 1) * m`;
  the last `n_steps % m` steps record none. `interval` must be an integer
  `>= 1`. The default is still 10, so a script that relied on per-step frames
  without asking for them must now pass `interval=1`.
- Recording moves no other number: the time series, DFT planes, flux monitors,
  port accumulators and S-parameters, and the final fields are bit-identical
  to the same run without a snapshot and at any other interval (CPU, tested
  with `np.array_equal`). An interval above 1 runs the steps in blocks of `m`
  and reads the frame from the carry after each block, so the step body is the
  one a run without a snapshot compiles; `interval=1` is recorded exactly as
  before.
- `Result.snapshot_axes[comp]` (`rfx.SnapshotAxes`) says where and when: the
  coordinate in metres of every sample along each kept axis (model frame, 0 at
  the domain's lower corner, CPML cells included), the slice plane, and the
  step count and time of each frame — E at `steps * dt`, H at
  `(steps - 1/2) * dt`. `slice_index` is a padded lattice index, the index
  space of `grid.position_to_index` and of `Result.state`.
  `rfx.snapshot_axes(grid, spec, n_steps)` gives the same before a run, and
  `rfx.field_sample_coords(grid, component)` gives the sample positions of the
  full arrays in `Result.state`.
- `run(..., until_decay=...)` now records snapshots too (it dropped them
  without a word). `save_snapshots(..., axes=result.snapshot_axes)` stores the
  positions and times and `load_snapshots` returns them as
  `metadata["axes"]`. `save_field_animation` titles each frame of a `Result`
  with its time.
- `stencil_order=4`: `Result.dt` is now the step the solver took
  (`0.857 * grid.dt`), not `grid.dt`. Every frequency read through it was
  0.857 of the true one: a 20 × 20 × 10 mm PEC cavity's TM110 (10.599 GHz)
  came out of `find_resonances` at 9.085 GHz. The time series itself is
  unchanged. `forward()` results gain the same `ForwardResult.dt` (their
  only step was `grid.dt`), and `save_simulation_dataset` writes that step
  as the file's `dt`.
- Uniform grid only: the non-uniform, distributed and subgridded lanes still
  warn and return no snapshot, and ADI refuses one. With low-level
  `run(..., checkpoint_segments=K)` the interval must divide `n_steps / K`.
