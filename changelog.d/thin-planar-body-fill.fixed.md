### Fixed — A dielectric slab or disc thinner than a cell read the wrong permittivity under subpixel smoothing (#PR)

Under `subpixel_smoothing=True` (uniform and graded mesh) and `"kottke_pec"`, a `Box`, or a `Cylinder` cut cap to cap, with both faces inside one cell's averaging length was smoothed as if it had one face: its fill and the medium outside it were taken from the nearer face only. On a uniform mesh an εr 4 slab 0.3 of a cell thick read Ez 1.429 where the series average is 1.290, and Ex 2.2 where the parallel average is 1.9; on a graded mesh Ez read 1.538. Such a body now reads its own thickness and the medium on both sides: Ez 1.290 on both meshes and Ex 1.9 on the uniform one, for host and traced builds. The derivative of that Ez with respect to a face moves from 3.06 to 2.50 per mm (uniform) and from 2.96 to 2.08 per mm (graded). Every other cell is unchanged bit for bit; a thin `Sphere`, a `Cylinder` crossed through its radius and `"full_tensor"` keep the earlier rule (see known limitations).

The permittivity build takes longer: on a 57×48×48 periodic scene a traced build went from 5.1 s to 6.6 s and a host build from 0.08 s to 0.12 s.
