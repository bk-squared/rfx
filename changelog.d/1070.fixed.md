### Fixed — a one-ULP excess in `domain/dx` no longer buys an unfilled cell at an absorber face (#1070)

- `Grid` sized itself with `int(np.ceil(domain[i] / dx))`. A declared domain
  length is an arithmetic expression, so that ratio can land one unit in the
  last place above an exact multiple — `232.00000000000003` for
  `38*h + 2*(10*h)` at `dx = h/4` — and `ceil` then bought a cell the declared
  domain does not reach. No `Box` fills it, and at a face abutting an absorber
  the CPML pad extension replicated that vacuum node through the whole pad, so
  the structure ended in a vacuum facet inside its own absorber. Sizing now
  goes through `rfx.grid.cells_spanning`, which takes a ratio within
  `8·eps·max(1, r)` of an integer to BE that integer. The tolerance is
  relative: an absolute one is coarser than an ULP at a million cells and finer
  than one at a few.
- The invariant behind the defect is now asserted at assembly rather than
  assumed. `rfx.geometry.rasterize_grid.assert_declared_span_is_filled` raises
  `PadFillShortfall` when a structure declared out to a padded face is
  rasterized more than one node short of it — one node being the documented
  half-open `[lo, hi)` shortfall the #627a fallback repairs. A box that does
  not reach the face, the common air gap before an absorber, is untouched.
- Measured build-only on the #801 patch rig (RO4003C, `h = 0.787 mm`,
  `dx = h/4`, 8 CPML layers, substrate across the whole declared domain): at
  +10h the x-hi pad read `eps_r = 1.0` with the last two interior nodes empty,
  and now reads 3.38 with one empty node. Realized grid `[250, 189, 81]` →
  `[249, 189, 81]`, which is what the issue's −1 ULP falsifier produced.
- No committed fixture moves: all 62 audited example-fidelity variants build to
  an identical `(nx, ny, nz)` before and after (48 through the uniform sizing
  path; the other 14 are non-uniform meshes, which `Grid` does not size).
- The same class of arithmetic in `rfx/nonuniform.py` (an absolute `1e-9` slack
  on a segment ratio at `:775`, `:843`, `:935`, and an absolute step tolerance
  at `:624`) is NOT changed here: those are per-segment cell counts rather than
  a domain length, and moving them moves non-uniform meshes. Filed as #1137.
