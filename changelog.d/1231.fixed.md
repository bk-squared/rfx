### Fixed — a thin-conductor sheet that reaches an absorbing face continues into it whatever shape it was drawn with (#1231)

- Since #1178 a conductor that reaches an absorbing face continues through the absorber, so a guide or a ground plane does not
  end in an open stub inside the CPML. A sheet drawn as a Box was continued. The same sheet drawn as another shape on the same
  cells stopped at the face, so a Leontovich parallel-plate guide read α = 0.680 Np/m instead of 0.750 depending only on how its
  plates were drawn. Every thin PEC or surface-impedance sheet now continues along its own plane, and both drawings give
  identical fields. Volumetric shapes that cannot be continued still stop at the face, with the warning.
- The Leontovich α regression pin moves from 0.698 to 0.750 Np/m, because the plates now continue into the source-side absorber
  (#1178 and this change) and the absorber's magnetic profile sits at the Yee half cell (#1012). The guide-fit √σ ratio pin
  moves from 0.609 to 0.597. The free-standing sheet still gives 0.50, so that fixture's √σ gate stays an expected failure.
