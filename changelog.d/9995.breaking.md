### BREAKING — a pulse that samples to zero at every time step is refused, not run (#9995)

- `preflight()`, `run()` and `forward()` now refuse a `GaussianPulse` or `ModulatedGaussian`
  source or port whose sampled drive has no value above 1e-6 of its amplitude. This happens when
  `bandwidth` is given in Hz instead of as a fraction: the pulse is then about 1e-20 s long and
  the run drove nothing (earlier releases warned and drove only the old pulse's jump at t = 0).
- What to do: pass a fractional `bandwidth`, e.g. `GaussianPulse(f0=5e9, bandwidth=0.8)`.

Upgrading to 2.0: a source whose `bandwidth` was given in Hz now raises instead of running.
