### Added — a design box may contain its own feed port: `forward(design_box_holds_ports=True)` (#1319)

- A permittivity `design_box` may now hold a lumped or wire port, on the uniform
  and the graded mesh. Each E edge the port drives or loads keeps the update and
  drive coefficients the drawn materials give it, 50 ohm load included: the design
  values do not reach it and its gradient is exactly zero. The port cells' other
  edges stay design variables.
- `ForwardResult.design_box_held_edges` lists the held edges as `(axis, i, j, k)`.
- Without the option a box holding a port still raises, and the message names it.
  A soft source, magnetic source or lumped RLC element in the box still raises.
