### Changed — the three coax calculators build their line section through one builder (#2940)

- `compute_coaxial_line_reflection`, `compute_coaxial_two_port` and `compute_coax_msl_transition` stamp the coax line,
  its feed resistors and its TEM plane sources through one function, `rfx.model.coax_line.build_coax_line`.
- No result changes: every array the three calculators pass to the time stepping and every S they return is
  byte-identical to before.
