### Fixed — far-field gradient memory when the direction budget splits (#1482)

- Under `jax.grad`, `compute_far_field_jax` now rebuilds each direction chunk's phase factor in the backward pass instead of keeping every chunk's factor. When `max_phase_bytes` splits the directions into 4 or 16 chunks, the far-field share of gradient memory falls to at most a quarter or a sixteenth of what was kept before.
