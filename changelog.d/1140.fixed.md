### Fixed — material assembly no longer asks a fresh simulation to plan its mesh (#1140, follow-up to #1070)

- #1136's assembly-time pad-fill check read `self._domain`, a mesh descriptor
  whose read resolves the mesh. `differentiable_material_fit` builds its grid
  once and then hands a fresh simulation carrying traced materials to
  `_assemble_materials` on every step; that simulation had never resolved its
  mesh, so the read ran the auto-mesh planner on a tracer and refused
  (`test_recover_debye_reference_mode_public_entry`, GPU suite 369367262302 on
  a6d6fce1; reproduced on CPU in 3.5 s). The check now takes the declared
  domain from `_declared_mesh` and skips when none was declared.
- A five-second test pins the invariant: assembling a never-resolved
  simulation with a grid built elsewhere reads no mesh field.
