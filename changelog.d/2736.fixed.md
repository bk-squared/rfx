### Fixed — An oblique Bloch plane wave with subpixel smoothing, conformal PEC or a Kottke PEC occupancy returned wrong fields without warning, and with a pole material it stopped with a raw error; both are now refused by name before any step (#2736)

The electric-field updates for these inputs do not apply the Bloch phase. On a 24 mm εr 4
slab at 30°, smoothing gave |T| 0.832 against the Airy value 0.749 (+0.91 dB); Debye and
Lorentz slabs stopped with a scan-carry `TypeError`. An oblique Bloch plane wave is now
refused with subpixel smoothing (`True`, `'full_tensor'`, `'kottke_pec'`), conformal PEC,
a PEC occupancy override with `pec_occupancy_model='kottke'`, Debye or Lorentz poles, or an
anisotropic permittivity tensor passed to the low-level `run`. The message names the input
and the routes that work: normal incidence, or that input turned off.
