### Fixed — traced pole-map profiles that are not finite outside their material keep finite gradients (#2736)

- A traced pole-map callable whose value or derivative is not finite outside its
  material no longer gives a NaN gradient on uniform or graded meshes.
- Float32 reverse-mode gradients of maps move at rounding level (7e-8 to 1e-6
  relative); an SI-scale map's float32 gradient moves by up to about 5e-3 relative,
  inside its underflow-limited error. Use `RelativeStrength` for traced SI-scale strengths.
- Forward values are unchanged.
