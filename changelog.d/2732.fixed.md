### Fixed — Closed-box plane-wave runs no longer slow down with the record length (#2732)

`add_tfsf_source(closed_box=True)` padded its incident line by the record length, so each
time step cost more the longer the run. The line now has a fixed length and ends in a
matched lossy layer. On one RTX 3090, a 500,000-step run of an empty 50,000-cell box takes
52 s instead of 116 s. The incident field now carries an echo from the line ends: within
the source's band it is at most about 1.3e-6 of the incident peak. A continuous wave or a
waveform with a DC offset leaves a larger, decaying difference near the line's highest
frequency, far above the band.
