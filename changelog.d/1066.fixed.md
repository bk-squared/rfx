### Fixed — the waveguide S-parameter lane continues a boundary-touching dielectric into its absorber pad (#1066)

- `compute_waveguide_s_matrix` rebuilt its own `shape_eps_pairs` from
  `sim._geometry` at both smoothing sites, under a comment saying it mirrored
  `rfx/runners/uniform.py`. Since #1043 the runners build theirs through
  `rfx.geometry.smoothing.smoothed_shape_pairs`, which continues a dielectric
  reaching a CPML/UPML face out through that pad; this lane did not, so such a
  structure was solved with `eps_r = 1` in its own absorber — the #831 end
  facet. Both sites now call the shared helper and handle `_unextendable` the
  way the runners do.
- Measured build-only on a WR-90 guide with an `eps_r = 4` slab running into
  the x-hi face: `eps_xx` read 1.0 down all 8 CPML cells before and 4.0 after,
  1539 of 13167 cells differing.
- No committed verdict moved. `subpixel_smoothing` defaults to False and both
  blocks sit behind it; an AST census of the whole tree finds 4 in-tree callers
  passing a truthy value — three fences that raise before the solve, and one
  real solve whose only interior geometry is a PEC box reaching no pad. The
  census classifies by callee name, so of 177 calls into the lane it reports 7
  explicit (the 4 plus 3 added here, build-only), 17 forwarding `**kwargs` and
  therefore UNKNOWN, and 153 passing neither.
  `scripts/diagnostics/waveguide_lane_pad_continuation_census.py` re-runs that
  census and the pad comparison.
