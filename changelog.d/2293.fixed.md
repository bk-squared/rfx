Correct dielectric subpixel smoothing on non-cubic and graded meshes to use
the averaging-cell extent along the interface normal, with primal widths
along each electric component and dual widths across it. Uniform cubic
results are unchanged. For a flat eps_r=4 slab, the previous normalization
shifted permittivity by up to 9.2% on constant non-cubic cells and 13.4% on
graded cells, with up to 26.1% variation along one flat interface.
These are material-assembly measurements; S-parameter effects are unmeasured.
