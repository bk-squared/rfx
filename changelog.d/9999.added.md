### Added — the far field from block current moments accumulated in the time loop: `Simulation.add_current_moment_monitor()` and `rfx.current_moment_far_field()` (#9999)

- A radiator's far field is what its current radiates. On the Yee lattice that current is
  `curl H - eps0 dE/dt` at every E edge, exactly zero in vacuum. The monitor sums it over a
  slab around the structure into 30 numbers per in-plane block (the total moment and its first
  and second moments about the block centre) and DFTs only those inside the time loop.
- `current_moment_far_field(result, theta, phi)` returns the `FarFieldResult` that
  `compute_far_field` returns for an NTFF box, so `directivity`, `radiation_pattern` and
  `axial_ratio` take it unchanged; under `jax.grad` and `jax.jit` it stays differentiable.
- Uniform and graded-z lanes, `run()` and `forward()`. The slab must be interior on a uniform
  in-plane mesh (dx = dy); periodic axes, TFSF, a traced mesh and the ADI, subgridded,
  multi-device and vmap lanes refuse the declaration.
