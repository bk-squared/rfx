### Added — a design box can carry one conductivity per E component, so a design sheet is a sheet and not a one-cell slab (#9999)

- `DesignBoxSpec.sigma` and `forward(design_sigma_override=)` now also take a
  3-tuple `(sigma_x, sigma_y, sigma_z)` of box-shaped arrays. A zero-thickness
  conducting sheet carries current along its two in-plane edges and none through
  its thickness; the box built one `(Ca, Cb)` pair from one conductivity array
  and applied it to ex, ey and ez alike, so a sheet declared as a design
  conductivity came out a lossy slab one cell thick. `_resolve_design_box` now
  builds one coefficient pair per component and `rfx.core.yee.update_e_box`
  routes component `c` to pair `c`. The permittivity stays shared — one material
  with an anisotropic current, not three materials — and `(s, s, s)` reproduces
  the single-array run bit for bit, fields and S-parameters. Measured on a
  6 × 5 mm patch drawn as a 1e5 S/m in-plane sheet: resonance within 0.0025 %
  and |S11| within 0.019 dB of the same patch drawn as pinned PEC, over
  10–13 GHz.
- `Result.wire_port_sparams` is filled on the graded-mesh lane, which built the
  per-port DFT accumulators already and then dropped them. An S-parameter is a
  ratio and carries no level, so a caller asking what fraction of the incident
  power a structure absorbed had no denominator. The field has the same shape
  on both lanes — a tuple of `(meta, accs)` pairs, `accs` being
  `(v, i, v_inc, v_port)` — and `forward()` surfaces it on the graded-mesh lane
  too.
- `forward(design_box=...)` no longer refuses a design box on the plane of a
  wire port's far terminal. A port from node `n0` to node `n1` drives the edges
  `n0 … n1 - 1`; node `n1` is where the current arrives and its plane holds no
  port cell. The declaration-level fence compared against `n1` and so rejected
  the patch metal of any patch fed from below by a via. It now compares against
  the last edge, matching the step-level fence and
  `rfx.sources.sources.wire_port_edge_span`.
