### Added — the far field from block current moments accumulated in the time loop: `Simulation.add_current_moment_monitor()` and `rfx.current_moment_far_field()` (#9999)

- A radiator's far field is what its current radiates: on the Yee lattice `curl H - eps0 dE/dt`
  at every E edge, zero in vacuum. The monitor sums it over a slab around the structure into 30
  numbers per in-plane block (total moment, first and second moments) and DFTs only those.
- `current_moment_far_field(result, theta, phi)` returns the `FarFieldResult` that
  `compute_far_field` returns for an NTFF box, so `directivity`, `radiation_pattern` and
  `axial_ratio` take it unchanged; under `jax.grad` and `jax.jit` it stays differentiable.
- Uniform and graded-z lanes, `run()` and `forward()`, order 2 only. Refused before the run: a
  slab not interior or not on a uniform in-plane mesh; mu_r != 1, PEC/PMC domain faces, plane
  ports, anything that carries current outside the slab; periodic axes, TFSF, `stencil_order=4`,
  a traced mesh; the ADI, subgridded, multi-device and vmap lanes.
