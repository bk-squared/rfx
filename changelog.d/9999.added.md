### Added — a record-length witness for gradients, the companion to the −40 dB settling witness for values (#9999, #1181)

- `gradient_record_length_witness(objective, params, n_steps, *, tol, factor=2.0)`
  differentiates the caller's objective at `n_steps` and again at
  `ceil(factor * n_steps)` and reports how far the gradient moved, per parameter
  leaf and per frequency bin, with the two records' settling levels beside it.
  A resonance still ringing at the end of the record leaves a term in every DFT
  bin whose phase is `(ω − ω_r)·T`; a parameter that moves the resonance spins
  that phase, and the spin enters the derivative magnified by the record length
  while the value hides it. Measured on a grounded-slab patch, a 2200-step
  record that passes the −40 dB settling rule gives |S11|² within 0.99 % and
  `d ln|S11|²/d ln εr` out by 9.7 … 23 %.
- `tol` is required and has no default: the bar depends on the structure's Q and
  on what the gradient is for. The docstring carries the measured sweep to
  calibrate against.
- Exported as `rfx.gradient_record_length_witness` /
  `rfx.GradientRecordLengthWitness`; `docs/guides/known_limitations.md` gains the
  matching entry.
