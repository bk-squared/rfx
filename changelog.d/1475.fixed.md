An oblique plane wave on the Bloch path (`add_tfsf_source(angle_deg=θ, method="bloch")`)
now warns when its pulse carries content at f_c = f0·sinθ above −60 dB of the level at f0.
That content does not leave the source plane, so a rectangular-window DFT of the probe
time series picks it up and a value at f0 changes with the record length (1.4 % at 43.6°
with bandwidth 0.3 in an empty box). The warning names the angle, bandwidth, f_c and level;
narrow the bandwidth or taper the tail of the time series before the DFT.
