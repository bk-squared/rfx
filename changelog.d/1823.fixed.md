### Fixed — Batch lumped RLC field updates (#1823)

Lumped RLC elements compute their corrections before updating each field
component together on uniform and graded meshes, preserving the element
equations and removing per-element field writes.
Two lumped RLC elements on the same edge are now refused, including folded
elements. An RLC element on a lumped or wire port edge is also refused.
Refusals name both conflicting declarations.
