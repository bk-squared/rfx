### Fixed — a 2-D `mode` combined with an axis profile returned zero fields (#1148)

- `Simulation(mode="2d_tez", dy_profile=…)` ran in the non-uniform lane, which
  is 3-D only and never read `mode`; on the one-cell PEC box only Ez survives,
  so every TEz field came back zero with no message. The constructor now
  refuses a 2-D mode together with `dx_profile` / `dy_profile` / `dz_profile`
  and names the way out: `mode="3d"`, a z extent of one or two cells, PEC z
  walls for TMz or magnetic z walls for TEz.
