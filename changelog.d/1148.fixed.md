### Fixed — a 2-D `mode` on a non-uniform mesh returned zero TEz fields (#1148)

- Any axis profile — passed in, invented by auto-meshing, or carried by a
  design document — sends a model to the non-uniform lane, which is 3-D only
  and never read `mode`. On the one-cell box a 2-D model declares only Ez
  survives, so `mode="2d_tez"` came back with every field zero (the only hint
  was the generic "source may not have coupled" warning). The lane dispatch
  now refuses `2d_tez`, and `2d_tmz` unless both z walls are PEC, and names
  the way out: `mode="3d"`, one cell between PEC z walls for TMz, TWO cells
  between magnetic z walls for TEz, or an explicit uniform `dx=`.
- `mode="2d_tmz"` with a profile between PEC z walls keeps running: it is
  byte-identical to the 3-D box and was always correct.
