### BREAKING — a driven lumped port reads its S11 from the terminal V/I pair, so every lumped `|S11|` changes (#1162)

- `add_port(..., extent=None)` sampled its physical V/I *before* source
  injection, read its diagonal with the passive port-branch algebra
  `(V + Z0·I)/(V - Z0·I)` on a driven port, and withheld the Yee half-step
  current phase. On a parallel-plate line terminated in a known resistor it
  returned `|S11|` 0.714 / 1.248 / 4.757 where the closed form
  `|(R - Zc)/(R + Zc)|` is 0.333 / 0 / 0.333 — the reciprocal of the physical
  reflection, non-passive on two of the three loads.
- A driven port now uses `(V - Z0·I)/(V + Z0·I)` on the post-injection pair,
  with the half-step phase on the H-derived current. The same cell declared as
  a one-cell wire port — which already had all three — now reads bit-identically
  to it. The wire family received these corrections in #683/#764 and the lumped
  family did not; nothing about the cell's sigma or its injection changed.
- **Every lumped-port `|S11|` a user has seen moves**, on
  `forward(port_s11_freqs=...)`, `run(compute_s_params=True)`,
  `compute_lumped_wire_s_matrix_via_scan(...)` and the eager
  `extract_s_matrix(...)`. Off-diagonal `S21` also moves, by the half-step
  current phase alone: the #308 incident-wave denominator still reads the
  pre-injection drive sample, now carried as its own channel.
- A passive (undriven) lumped port keeps the port-branch FORMULA, but its
  value moves: its voltage is bit-identical either side of the injection slot
  (measured, `max |V_post - V_pre| = 0.0` at a passive port), while its current
  now carries the Yee half-step phase. On a two-port PEC fixture the passive
  diagonal moves by `max |dS| = 0.026` and `max |d|S|| = 0.0022`.
- **A V/I dump written after this change carries a new channel.**
  `PortVIReplayBundle` and the `.npz` schema gained `drive_ref_voltages`, the
  pre-injection drive sample, because the production off-diagonal incident
  wave is built on it — the wire bundle got `raw_drive_ref_voltages_fdt` for
  the same reason. Without it a new dump replays its diagonal and gets its
  off-diagonals wrong and sign-flipped (`max_abs_diff 0.0272` against a
  `8.4e-07` tolerance on the two-port fixture).
- Dumps written BEFORE this change replay unchanged, and that is now pinned by
  a committed fixture rather than argued:
  `tests/fixtures/lumped_two_port_vi_dump_pre_driven_diagonal.npz`, written by
  the old extractor on b4cf8f29, replays to its own recorded S-matrix to
  `8.7e-08`. Absent `diagonal_frame` selects the port-branch diagonal and
  absent `drive_ref_voltages` selects `voltages` as the incident wave, which
  is what that dump's production S used.
- The experimental subgridded runner's diagnostic lumped S-matrix is the one
  lane not moved — its sampling slot depends on
  `inject_sources_before_e_coupling`, so neither the slot nor the dt/2 phase is
  derivable there. It now warns, with its own
  `PreDecisionLumpedDiagonalWarning` category, that it is on the old
  convention; `tests/contracts/test_pre_decision_lumped_diagonal_is_announced.py`
  pins that the guard stays.
- The mixed lumped+MSL lane keeps its own (refuted) driven diagonal, which is
  cross-family work the PI deferred, and now warns with the same
  `PreDecisionLumpedDiagonalWarning` when its feed is lumped. Its drive
  reference reads the pre-injection channel explicitly, so what the #488
  algebra was calibrated against did not move underneath it; its current did,
  by the half-step phase. Measured before/after: `max |dS| = 0.0` on the
  committed fixtures (they drive a WIRE feed, which this change does not
  touch) and `max |dS| = 1.8e-05`, `max |d|S|| = 1.7e-07` on a lumped-feed
  variant of the same fixture.
- Pinned by `tests/unit/ports/test_lumped_port_known_load_line.py` against the
  closed form, not against rfx's own helpers;
  `scripts/diagnostics/lumped_port_known_load_line.py` is the decision run.
