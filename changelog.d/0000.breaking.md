The coaxial lanes realize the pin and the outer conductor as PEC edge masks
through `realized_pec_edge_masks`, not as `sigma = PEC_SIGMA` per node, and the
wall's inner radius is the declared outer radius with a thickness fixed in
metres rather than one cell. **Every coaxial S-parameter a user has seen moves.**

`rfx/core/yee.py` applies `sigma` to the three E components co-indexed with a
node, so a stamped conductor cell damped only its three plus-side edges and the
edges entering it from the minus side stayed live on the neighbouring dielectric
node. That one-sided wall carried extra inductance and its rasterized ring leaked
at the corner contacts. Measured on the through line at 4 annulus cells: the
fitted phase constant sat 18.7 % above `omega sqrt(eps_r)/c` and 12 % of the
column power was missing; both are now within 1 % and 0.4 %. The wall's inner
radius used to be `b - min(dx, (b-a)/2)`, so the dielectric annulus — and the
line's own characteristic impedance — moved with the mesh (40.7 to 45.3 ohm over
a 4-to-9-annulus-cell ladder against a declared 48.59); it no longer does.

`stamp_coaxial_line` and `stamp_coaxial_short_plane` now return the conductor
cells alongside the materials, and `stamp_coaxial_annular_resistor` takes that
mask instead of testing `sigma >= PEC_SIGMA/2`. `compute_coaxial_s_matrix` is
deprecated and unchanged.
