### Fixed — a lumped port's load or a folded R / C loaded all three E edges at its node; it now loads its own edge only (#1236)

- A lumped element folded into the material — the load of a lumped, wire or
  MSL port, a parallel R or C, a single-component series element — is a device
  across one Yee edge. Its stamp carried no component and the E update added it
  to Ex, Ey and Ez at the node, so a 50 Ω port on Ez was also a 50 Ω resistor on
  the Ex and Ey edges leaving its node. On a centre-fed half-wave dipole
  (L = 47.5 mm) the feed-tip radial field on those two sides was pinned to 0.03
  of the gap field against 0.37 on the free side; the resonance read +0.34 %
  high at λ/43 (+0.16 % at λ/85) and Zin was off by 12–28 % at 3.5–4 GHz. A lone
  one-cell 50 Ω port in free space read Re Zin ≈ 15 Ω where its radiation
  resistance is below 1 Ω, and in a closed PEC box it read +15 Ω below the
  first cavity mode, where a lossless box has none.
- Where the node carries no transverse field nothing moves: a patch fed by a
  50 Ω wire port across its substrate (1e-7 in the dip frequency), the one-cell
  parallel-plate line of the lumped/wire battery (bitwise), an MSL thru line
  (|ΔS| ≤ 2.6e-5).
- `MaterialArrays.sigma_lumped` / `eps_r_lumped` are now `None` or a
  per-component 3-tuple `(x, y, z)`; `stamp_lumped_sigma` /
  `stamp_lumped_eps` take the element's component as a fourth argument, and a
  component-less record raises `TypeError`. Every per-component lane reads the
  new rule (uniform, graded mesh, subgridding, GPU bake, design box, drive
  coefficients, series/parallel element `D0`), and the distributed slab update
  keeps each stamp on its own edge. The Debye/Lorentz E update is still
  cell-owned and says so with a warning when a lumped element is present.
  `save_materials` / `load_materials` keep the records.
- Closed boxes lose damping they should never have had: a lossless cavity fed
  by a port now rings longer, so a record that settled before may not.
