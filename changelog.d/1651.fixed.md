`compute_mixed_s_matrix` now refuses every lumped- or wire-driven column
and its power witnesses, in both magnitude channels. MSL-driven values
remain unprojected; joint passivity projection is skipped while entries
are refused. Multi-cell wire receive waves are also NaN, including in
`S_wave`; use the flux channel for their magnitudes. For a lumped or wire
feed, use uniform `run(compute_s_params=True)` without MSL ports, or use
MSL ports only. Warnings name the refused entries and alternatives.
