`compute_mixed_s_matrix` now returns NaN for driven lumped reflections and
the power witnesses and flux magnitudes that depend on them. It warns which
entries are refused and skips joint passivity projection. Wave transmission
and MSL-driven columns keep their unprojected values; wire mode is unchanged.
For lumped reflection, use `run(compute_s_params=True)` or
`compute_lumped_wire_s_matrix_via_scan` on a lumped-only model.
