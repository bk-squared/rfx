### Fixed — Wire ports with a radius admit pole materials away from the pin (#2972)

- `add_port(..., radius=...)` now runs when Debye, Lorentz or Drude material cells stay outside the port's electric
  stencil, on uniform and graded Yee meshes and the distributed paths that support radius.
- Pole cells that reach the live pin edges, their four parallel neighbours, or the cells averaged onto those edges are
  still refused, with an error naming the port and the cell.
