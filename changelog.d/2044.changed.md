### Changed — the settling check identifies long windows with a partial SVD (#2044)

- After a run, the ring-down settling check identifies poles with a partial
  SVD, accepted only when its residual is within the rounding bound of the
  Hankel products; otherwise it uses the full SVD, or refuses as before.
- WR-90 waveguide port, 131,328 steps: the pencil on the 98,496-sample window
  takes 2.0 s instead of 30.1 s (median of 5), with the same rank and verdict,
  and the window is judged under the default 4 GiB bound, which refused it.
- `rfx.ringdown.identify`, `rfx.matrix_pencil` and every pre-run check keep
  the full SVD. Ring-down completion in `run(ringdown=)` and
  `forward(ringdown=)` still refuses those windows before the run.
