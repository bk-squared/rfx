### Added — the geometry the grid actually built is one public record (#1440, #1138)

- `Simulation.realized_geometry()` and `Result.realized_geometry` give, per entity, the realized node and cell ranges, extents
  and per-face residual against the drawing (a PEC sheet at its solved edge), wall planes, port drive edges and apertures,
  and the domain's realized cell count and padding.
- `fidelity_report()`, preflight and the tutorials now read this one record instead of re-deriving geometry, so the report
  shows what the solver uses: a strip drawn to a domain face that is not a whole number of cells is reported continued to
  the realized face, as the solver builds it.
