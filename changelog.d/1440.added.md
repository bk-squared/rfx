### Added — public realized geometry (#1440, #1138)

- `Simulation.realized_geometry()` exposes entity ranges, solved sheet spans, residuals, counts, planes, ports and domain padding.
- `Result.realized_geometry` summarizes the run's own assembly without retaining dense masks or material arrays.
- Diagnostic masks are available on request through `Simulation.realized_geometry()`; geometry, material, mesh and port changes invalidate its cache.
- Fidelity and tutorial readers use the public record. A port-held conductor is reported at its assembled termination.
- Subgrid results identify the coarse lane and explicitly mark the refined region as unrepresented.
