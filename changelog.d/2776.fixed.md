### Fixed — refuse lumped elements shorted by PEC (#2776)

- A lumped RLC element on a declared conductor’s PEC edge previously had no effect.
  Runs and forwards now refuse it by name before stepping, with a gap or placement remedy.
  Elements on declared PEC wall edges are also refused. Elements normal to a sheet,
  and edges released by a lumped port, remain admitted. Design occupancy retains
  its existing keep-free behavior.
