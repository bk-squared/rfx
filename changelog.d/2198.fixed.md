### Fixed — CPU gradients through a CPML absorber no longer sweep the whole domain for every absorber correction (#2198)

- In v2.0.0rc2 the reverse pass of each time step on CPU wrote every CPML correction back across the whole domain several
  times, so one gradient took 2.6 times v1.8.0's time (531,441-cell box, 400 checkpointed steps, 8 cores: 27.0 s against
  10.2 s). The write-back now has its own derivative rule whose reverse touches only the absorber slab. The forward
  computation is the same code, and GPU gradients keep their write-back unchanged.
