### Fixed — the near-to-far-field transform is second order in the cell size, and its E field is stamped at the right time (#1159)

- The Huygens-box surface integral took all four tangential components of a
  face where the Yee lattice stores them and priced every one of them at the
  cell's lower-corner node — a left-endpoint rectangle rule, first order in
  the cell size, with the tangential H half a cell off the face along its
  normal. Each component is now moved to the CENTRE of its face cell before
  it enters the running DFT (an exact midpoint average between two nodes in
  the plane, a linear interpolation across the face along the normal using
  the two adjacent cell widths), and `compute_far_field` /
  `compute_far_field_jax` price every sample at that centre. On a closed-form
  dipole oracle the complex relative error falls by 4x per halving of the
  surface mesh instead of 2x: 1.48e-02 / 3.69e-03 / 9.22e-04 at lambda/10,
  /20, /40 against 3.17e-01 / 1.60e-01 / 8.01e-02 before.
- The E field was stamped at `n*dt` while the state handed to
  `accumulate_ntff` holds E at `(n+1)*dt`, so E ran half a step BEHIND H
  instead of half a step ahead. Both fields are now stamped with their own
  sample time.
- The scalar area element returned `dx*dy` for every face. That is the wrong
  pair for a y face, which is spanned by x and z, on any grid whose y cells
  differ from its z cells. Latent: not reachable from `Simulation`, whose
  plain `Grid` is cubic and whose `NonUniformGrid` always carries per-cell
  arrays and takes the full per-cell branch.
- Measured on production fixtures: the Hertzian z-dipole directivity moves
  from 1.8000 to 1.7614 dBi against a theoretical 1.7609 (error 0.039 dB to
  0.0005 dB), the half-wave dipole from 2.380 to 2.156 dBi against 2.15, and
  the NTFF-to-flux-box radiated-power ratio from 0.4948 to 0.5003 against a
  derived exactly 0.5.
- `NTFFBox` gained a `face_centre` flag (default `False`, the pre-existing
  layout) and a read-only `collocation` property that names it (`"node"` /
  `"face_centre"`), so accumulators dumped by an earlier run are read back
  with the geometry they were accumulated with. Every path that builds a box
  for a new run sets it. The flag is a bool, not a string, so the box stays a
  valid JAX pytree.
