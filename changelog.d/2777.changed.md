### Changed
- On GPUs of compute capability 8.6 (RTX A6000, RTX 3090) with jax 0.10.2, a uniform single-device
  run whose cell count is a multiple of 4 while its z cell count is not now steps 2.6 to 2.8 times
  faster (measured at 364 x 365 x 113, 364 x 365 x 29 and a box with a port at 364 cells in x). The time
  step pads z to a multiple of 4 inside the step; the grid, the fields and every reported number stay
  as they were: one step is bitwise or within 9 float32 last bits, and what a result reads within 1e-4.
  Memory grows by up to 3/nz on those grids. Other devices, other jax versions, graded, multi-device and
  subgridded runs are not padded.
