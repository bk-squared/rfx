### Changed — the uniform time step pads z on compute capability 8.6 where the update loop was slow (#2777)

- On RTX A6000 and RTX 3090 GPUs (compute capability 8.6) with jax 0.10.2, a uniform single-device run whose cell count is a multiple of 4 while its z cell count is not steps 2.6 to 2.8 times faster (364 x 365 x 113, 364 x 365 x 29, and a box with a port at 364 cells in x).
- The step pads z to a multiple of 4 inside the time step. The grid, the fields and every reported number stay as they were: one step is bitwise or within 9 float32 last bits, and what a result reads is within 1e-4.
- Memory grows by up to 3/nz on those grids. Other devices, other jax versions, graded, multi-device and subgridded runs are not padded.
