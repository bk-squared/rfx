### Fixed — settling warnings name a pole that grows after the source ends instead of advising a longer record (#3064)

- The record-end settling witness lists each growing pole once (conjugates and channels of one pole grouped): frequency, growth per step, e-folding steps and largest share at the read bins.
- When such a pole fails the witness, the warning names it and says that a longer record carries a larger error from it; it no longer advises a larger `n_steps` or `num_periods` for that row. Rows without a growing pole keep their text.
- Known limitations: a dielectric near a CPML face can hold a guide field that grows after the source ends. The CPML docstring now states the grading-order default the code uses, 3 (cubic).
