PEC/PMC wall axes now use the nearest whole-cell length on uniform and graded meshes.
Absorber axes use the cells needed to span the declaration in both mesh types;
periodic counts and explicit graded profiles are unchanged.
Simulation refuses wall-length changes over 1% with `snap="strict"`;
`snap="declared"` accepts them with a warning naming alternative cell sizes.
WR-90 at 1.5 mm used to be solved 24.0 mm wide, putting its TE10 cutoff 4.75% low.
It is now refused by default, or solved 22.5 mm wide with a warning.
Automatic spacing fits walls and periods together without coarsening, refuses
refinement below 75% of the requested spacing, and suggests a fitting explicit size.
Direct low-level grid builders apply the count rule without Simulation's snap judgement.
