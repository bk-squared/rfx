### Changed — one DFT and one time stamp for every stored spectrum (#1526)

- Every stored spectrum is stamped with the time its field holds: after step
  n, E at (n+1)·dt and H at (n+½)·dt. Only the absolute phase moves:
  - by −ω·dt: port V and I spectra (lumped and wire, uniform and graded mesh),
    and on a graded mesh E-field DFT planes and the flux monitor's E and H;
  - by +ω·dt/2: H-field DFT planes on a uniform mesh and in a vmap sweep;
    by −ω·dt/2: H-field DFT planes on a graded mesh.
- Magnitudes and every ratio (S, Z, wave amplitudes, flux, gain) are unchanged
  at the 1e-4 level.
- A `precision="float64"` run weights the port DFT in complex128: S moves by
  at most 5.6e-5 of peak at weakly driven bins and 1.4e-6 at strong bins.
