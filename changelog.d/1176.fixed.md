### Fixed — a far-field box that does not enclose the plane-wave injection region is refused instead of returning a wrong cross-section (#1176)

- A total-field/scattered-field source injects between two node planes on each
  axis it drives; inside them the grid carries incident + scattered field,
  outside only scattered. The far-field integral is over the scattered field
  alone, which it is only when the Huygens box surrounds the whole injected
  region. A box with a face ON an injection plane, or INSIDE the injected
  region, was accepted and returned a wrong cross-section from a run that
  finished normally with no warning. On an 18 mm eps_r = 4 cube at
  9/10/11 GHz with dx = 3 mm, against a clean box: +20.8 dB with the high face
  on the plane, +6.1 dB with one face one cell inside it (+16.2 dB for the same
  box under `collocation="node"`), and a box wholly inside reports
  1.94e-03 m^2 of backscatter from an empty domain.
- Both runners and `compute_rcs` now refuse such a box before the time loop,
  naming the face, its index and the index to move to; `compute_rcs` also
  reports the `ntff_offset`, `tfsf_margin` and `cpml_layers` that produced it,
  since its caller never chose an index. `compute_rcs(ntff_offset=0)` and
  oblique runs with `ntff_offset >= tfsf_margin - 1` are refused for this
  reason; their previous results were wrong.
- The oblique open-domain source injects on a rectangle, so its y faces are
  checked as well as its x faces. The normal-incidence source's slab is
  infinite in y and z, so those faces are not checked — that remains
  `compute_rcs(..., subtract_incident_reference=True)`'s business.
