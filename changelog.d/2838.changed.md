### Changed — `rfx.subgridding` states its conventions in docstrings instead of code comments (#2838)

- Six `rfx.subgridding` modules have no code comments left. Some of the sign, staggering, unit and source
  conventions and the meanings of some values they held now sit in the docstrings of the functions and classes
  they describe, at most one sentence each, so `help()` shows them; the other comments were removed.
- No computation changes: every result of the subgridding lane is the same as before.
