### Fixed — find_resonances reports only resonances the record contains (#9998)
`find_resonances`, `matrix_pencil_from_probe`, and `harminv_from_probe` no longer add aliased modes from long records or FFT-grid modes from short windows; no in-band modes means `[]`.
The shared ring-down window removes the mean and truncates time when anti-aliased decimation would retain more than 10,000 samples.
Direct fits that found a mode with at most 10,000 retained samples return the same modes as before.
`bandpass=True` and `max_samples` are ignored and emit deprecation warnings.
