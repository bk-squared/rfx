### Fixed — the MSL `Z0` guard and preflight now say the same measured thing about probe clearance (#726)

- They contradicted each other on the same run. `compute_msl_s_matrix`'s guard
  said "The V·I-split S11/S21 are unaffected"; preflight said the same
  condition would make a physical `|S11|` → 1 "read as -5 to -10 dB instead of
  0 dB". Which was true decided whether a class of results was readable.
- The measurement settles it (VESSL 369367260508, cv06b fixed-source, same
  source/load/DUT with only the p1 observation offset varied, both arms settled
  below −118 dB): the FITTED `Z0`/`beta` are what corrupt — the near-reflector
  arm's β scan railed on 51/51 bins over 3–5 GHz against 0/51 for the compliant
  arm — while raw `S11` at the 3.77125 GHz notch bin read +0.026895 dB on the
  near-reflector arm and +0.018065 dB on the compliant one, against the
  analytic 0 dB that notch has. Both sit above unity on a passive structure, by
  0.31 % and 0.21 %, which is never reported as physics here: they are raw,
  unprojected, and carry the coherent power excess tracked as #838. Their
  0.009 dB difference is one fixture, one bin and an arm-to-arm difference, not
  a bound on `S`, and the producer verdict was `not_read`. The `-5 to -10 dB`
  figure came from the shorted-line ladder withdrawn on 2026-09-13.
- Both sites, and the auto-offset resolver's "interval empty" warning, now
  embed one shared text (`rfx.preflight.msl.MSL_PROBE_CLEARANCE_EFFECT` and
  `MSL_PROBE_CLEARANCE_GUIDANCE`) rather than each carrying its own prose, and
  tests fail if either drops it or revives a retired claim. The `Z0` guard
  appends it only when that port's `probe_clearance` is NOT satisfied: on a
  satisfied port the guard's common trigger is coarse-mesh staircase bias
  (#752), and "lengthen the feed" is the wrong remedy for it.
- Two PUBLIC guide pages still narrated the retired claim and are rewritten; a
  contract test now scans `docs/public/guide/*.mdx` and `docs/guides/*.md` for
  both retired phrases, so prose about these messages cannot drift from them
  again.
- `preflight_sparameters(calculator="msl")` reports the condition. It used to
  return zero findings on a port whose deepest probe sat inside a reflector,
  while `preflight()` on the same simulation returned the layout warning and
  the result's `probe_clearance` read `insufficient`.
- `MSLSMatrixResult.probe_clearance` / `MixedSMatrixResult.probe_clearance` and
  the S-parameter support matrix now say what to gate on: `probe_clearance` for
  the geometry, `beta_railed` for the fitted-value symptom, never `reliable`,
  and what to do when no compliant offset exists on the feed at all.
