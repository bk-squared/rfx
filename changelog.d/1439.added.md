### Added — uniform H-plane and far-field adjoint objectives (#1439)

- `forward(gradient="adjoint")` accepts H DFT planes and NTFF far-field objectives on uniform meshes, including NTFF-only measurements. It retains the settled-record requirement and identical interior monitor frequency bins.
- On an RTX A6000 in float32, the adjoint gradient took 1.7–1.9× less time and 2.3–4.1× less device memory than reverse mode on a 5.1e5-cell and a 3.7e7-cell antenna scene, and its memory does not grow with the record length.
