### Added — Progress events as data (#2417)

Use `RFX_EVENTS=stderr` or `rfx.progress.events(sink)` with a callable or file
path to receive versioned events for uniform/graded runs and waveguide, MSL,
mixed and lumped/wire S-matrix phases and drive passes. Each `RFX_EVENT ` line
fits in 500 bytes. Human progress text remains controlled by `report_every`.

Compile reports `compile_not_separated`; multi-device/subgridded runs, eager
forward and coaxial calculators report `path_not_wired`. Transformed forward
reports `traced` once per entry/path. Graded calls without `report_every`
retain their existing scan and mark the step phase `ticks: false`.
