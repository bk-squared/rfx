### Fixed — graded multi-device runs use the one-device material coefficients (#9996, #1373)

On a GPU, 2- or 3-device graded runs with concrete (not traced) materials now step with the same permittivity, conductivity and permeability coefficients as one device.
Previously, dielectric corner coefficients could be 1 float32 bit lower, moving probe records by up to about 70 float32 ULP of the peak (S-parameters about 2e-7).
Traced materials are unchanged. The host holds the whole-grid material arrays while they are formed.
