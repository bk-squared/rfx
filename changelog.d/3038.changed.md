### Changed — The `accuracy` levels of the auto-configuration no longer state an error percentage (#3038)

- The docstring of `auto_configure` gave about 10 %, 3 % and 1 % error for
  `accuracy="draft"`, `"standard"` and `"high"`. No test measures the error at
  those resolutions, so the percentages are removed; a mesh series decides the
  accuracy. The cell counts per wavelength and the margins are unchanged.
