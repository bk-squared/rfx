### BREAKING — A traced permittivity that puts another medium at a waveguide port's S21 probe plane now raises (#2016)

`compute_waveguide_s_matrix` with a traced `eps_override` (for example under `jax.grad`) whose medium at a port's S21
probe plane differs from that port's section now raises `ValueError`. Before, it returned S and its gradient with the
vacuum split of S21, which is wrong for a dielectric at that plane. Keep a traced design region between the two ports'
probe planes and off their sections.
