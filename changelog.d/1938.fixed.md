### Fixed — Raw tracer errors and silent skips on traced inputs become named refusals, checks or warnings (#1938)

These used to stop with a raw JAX conversion error deep inside model assembly, or say nothing. They now refuse by name
before the time loop, run a check, or warn:
- a wire port beside a conductor on a traced mesh under `jit`/`vmap` (also `optimize(jit=True)`; eager differentiation
  still works);
- a flux monitor whose cells are traced;
- a PEC `Box` with a traced extent and a PEC `Sphere` with a traced radius under `jit`/`vmap`.
`make_nonuniform_grid` with a traced `dz` now checks that the cells add up to the declared z extent (it skipped the check;
#1686). Ring-down identification probes that duplicate one cell and component warn once. `_nominal_profiles` is no longer
listed as exported by the design interop.
