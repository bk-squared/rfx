Off-grid geometry is checked against the dimensions realized by the solver.
Metal dimensions missing their drawing by more than 1% refuse under strict snap
and warn under declared snap; material volumes warn in either mode.
Material extent and centroid differences share one message per volume.
Graded x/y profiles are checked against the declared domain length.
Points beyond the interior refuse on uniform and graded meshes.
Smoothed volumes and conformal open areas use integral comparisons.
An unavailable analytic integral reference is explicitly reported as not judged.
Standalone preflight reads the same verdict; skipping preflight does not skip it.
Existing preflight diagnostic codes and ordering are retained.
Periodic extents compare wrapped occupancy; wall faces clip and absorber faces continue.
Wire-port lengths include the full driven edges, including a single-cell port.
