### Fixed — Wire ports with a radius admit pole materials away from the pin (#2968)

Wire ports with `radius=` now run when declared Debye, Lorentz or Drude
material cells stay outside the port's electric stencil, on uniform and
graded Yee meshes, including supported distributed paths.
Pole cells reaching the live pin edges, their four parallel neighbours or
the cells supplying those edges' material means still refuse the solve.
The error names the port, the intersecting cell and the missing dispersive
storage correction.
