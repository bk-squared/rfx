### Fixed — an MSL or coaxial port on a traced mesh is refused by name (#2840)

- A model with an MSL or coaxial port whose `dx_profile`, `dy_profile` or `dz_profile` is traced under `jax.jit` or `jax.grad` is now refused with `TracedStructureRefusal`, naming the traced axes, when `run()`, `forward()` or an S-parameter entry admits the line ports.
- Before, a strip drawn with `add_pinned_sheet` stopped with a raw JAX `ConcretizationTypeError`. With preflight on, a Box strip stopped with a raw `TracerArrayConversionError`. No such model ran before or runs now; only the error changes.
