### Fixed — a microstrip port's automatic probe ladder is the right length on a graded runway (#1278, #810)

- The automatic `n_probe_offset` of `add_msl_port` clears two lengths from the
  source, the fringing transient (5·h_sub) and λ_eff/(4π) at `freq_max`, and the
  automatic `n_probe_spacing` keeps the ladder span at λ_eff/8. They were counted
  in the simulation's scalar cell, so on a propagation axis graded to a finer
  runway they realized a fraction of each length: on a 254 µm board with a
  127 µm runway under a 254 µm boundary cell, probe 0 sat 635 µm from the source
  instead of the 1.270 mm the fringing needs. The S-parameter drivers now count
  the stored lengths in the runway cell at the source node (10 cells = 1.270 mm
  there) when the whole probe ladder lies in one uniform zone. A ladder that
  would cross a grading ramp keeps the stored counts, and the existing warning
  says so. The reflector interval solve (#469) and the spacing widening (#681)
  still do not run on a graded axis. Uniform meshes and explicit values are
  unchanged.
- `sim.preflight()` and the S-parameter routing check now say what leaving an
  explicit offset `None` would give using the same floor as `add_msl_port`,
  including the λ_eff/(4π) term, and name the term that set the automatic
  offset. They used to judge `None` on 5·h_sub alone.
