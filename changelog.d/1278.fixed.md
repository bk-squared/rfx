### Fixed — a microstrip port's automatic probe ladder is the right length on a graded runway (#1278, #810)

- On a mesh graded to a finer runway under the port, the automatic
  `n_probe_offset` and `n_probe_spacing` of `add_msl_port` were counted in the
  coarser boundary cell. On a 127 µm runway under a 254 µm boundary cell, probe 0
  sat 635 µm from the source, inside the 1.270 mm the source fringing needs. It now
  sits at 1.270 mm, and the ladder spans λ_eff/8 as on a uniform mesh. A ladder
  that would cross a grading ramp keeps the old counts, with a warning.
- `sim.preflight()` now says what leaving the offset `None` would give using the
  same floor as `add_msl_port`, including the λ_eff/(4π) term. Uniform meshes and
  explicit offsets are unchanged.
