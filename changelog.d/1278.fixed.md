### Fixed — a microstrip port's automatic probe ladder is the right length on a graded runway (#1278, #810)

- The automatic `n_probe_offset` and `n_probe_spacing` of `add_msl_port` were
  counted in the scalar `dx`, not in the cells under the port. On a 127 µm runway
  under a 254 µm `dx` (graded, or a uniform `dy_profile`), probe 0 sat 635 µm from
  the source, inside the 1.270 mm the source fringing needs. It now sits at
  1.270 mm, and the ladder spans λ_eff/8 as on a uniform mesh. A ladder that would
  cross a grading ramp keeps the old counts, with a warning.
- `sim.preflight()` now says what leaving the offset `None` would give using the
  same floor as `add_msl_port`, including the λ_eff/(4π) term. Meshes whose cell
  is `dx`, and explicit offsets, are unchanged.
