### Fixed — a float64 run built its material coefficients in float32 (#2724)

- With `precision="float64"` inside `jax.enable_x64()`, permittivity, conductivity, permeability, Kerr chi3, Drude and
  Lorentz pole weights, conformal-PEC edge fractions and the uniform current-source coefficient are now built in
  float64 on the uniform and graded single-device meshes. Before, they carried float32 rounding of up to about 1e-7
  relative (conductivity 1.03e-7, pole weights 9.8e-8, graded permittivity 8.3e-8, conformal fractions 5.0e-8).
- The uniform mesh's current-source amplitude was 4.55e-8 off the graded mesh's on identical cells; the two now agree
  to about 5e-12. A per-cell pole map on a graded float64 mesh now matches the scalar pole within 1e-9 (was 5e-8).
- Float32 and mixed-precision runs are unchanged bit for bit. In a float64 run these material arrays use twice the
  memory.
