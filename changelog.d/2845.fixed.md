### Fixed — Second derivatives through forward(ringdown=) are NaN with a named warning, not a finite value that left out the pole terms (#2845)

- Second derivatives of the completed spectrum (`jax.hessian`, or `jax.grad`/`jvp`/`jacfwd`/`jacrev` nested) through `forward(ringdown=)` now return NaN with `rfx.errors.RingdownSecondDerivativeWarning`. Before, they returned a finite value without the terms through the identified poles: 0.32 of the peak off on a reproduced case.
- An entry of a joint second-order form that needs only the completed spectrum's first derivative is exact or NaN, never another finite number.
- First derivatives and every derivative of the in-record spectrum are exact and unchanged.
- `warnings.filterwarnings("error", category=RingdownSecondDerivativeWarning)` turns the NaN into an exception.
