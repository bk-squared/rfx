### BREAKING — MSL and coax port S is referenced to the declared impedance=, not the line's analytic Z0 (#9991)

MSL and coax port S is now referenced to the declared `impedance=` (default 50 ohm),
not the line's analytic Z0 (Hammerstad-Jensen for MSL, TEM for coax). This holds for
`compute_msl_s_matrix`, `compute_mixed_s_matrix`, `compute_coax_msl_transition` and
`compute_coaxial_two_port`. On a 52.9 ohm line a stub-matched dip moves 34 MHz and its
depth from -117 to -31 dB; away from it |S11| moves up to 0.4 dB. The line Z0 stays
reported as `line_impedances`; `reference_impedances` and `z0_ref` now hold the
declared values. A port whose `impedance=` equals its line Z0 returns the same S as before.
