### Changed — `sim.run(devices=...)` exchanges only the four boundary faces the Yee update reads, and sums the probes once after the time loop (#1222)

- Every step used to send all six field components across every slab cut in both
  directions (with wrap-around), then all-reduce the probe samples: for two devices, 24
  face transfers and 13 collectives per step. The E update reads only Hy and Hz from the
  left neighbour and the H update only Ey and Ez from the right one, so the runner now
  sends those two pairs, one packed transfer each way: 4 face transfers and 2 collectives
  per step. Probe samples stay on their owner device and are summed once after the loop.
- On two RTX 4090 nodes the time loop runs about 3× faster per step (2.7–3.5×, measured
  before and after on the same node pair: 11.1 → 4.1 ms at 10.8 M cells, 297 → 90 ms at
  324 M cells). Inside one node (three A6000) the gain is 6 %.
- Forward values are bit-identical to the full exchange on JAX 0.10.2 and 0.6.2 (PEC and
  CPML; 2, 3 and 4 devices with uneven slabs; seam-cell sources and probes; a PMC face, a
  PEC volume, a lossy block, Debye and Lorentz blocks and a lumped port), and on GPU for
  the two models measured there (a vacuum box on two nodes; a lossy block with Debye and
  Lorentz poles on three A6000). On JAX 0.4.33 (CPU) the model with all of those features
  and PEC walls moves by 1–2 float32 ULP because the compiler fuses the shorter program
  differently. Gradients through a traced source amplitude move by 1–2 float32 steps.
- Trace contexts: on the CI build (JAX 0.6.2) and on 0.10.2, the plain call, `jax.jvp`,
  `jax.vjp`, `jax.value_and_grad`, a `jax.vmap` member and `stop_gradient` give the same
  bits for a box with sources and probes in the cells on either side of a slab cut, as on
  main (`tests/unit/runners/test_distributed_v2_cross_trace.py`). On JAX 0.4.33 (CPU) the
  `vmap` member of the PEC model moves by 1–2 float32 steps where main keeps it; per the
  PI's 2026-09-23 ruling the rule binds the CI build.
- The multi-device `forward(distributed=True)` path still uses the full exchange.
