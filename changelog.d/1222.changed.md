### Changed — `sim.run(devices=...)` exchanges only the four boundary faces the Yee update reads, and sums the probes once after the time loop (#1222)

- Each step sent all six field components across every slab cut in both directions and then
  all-reduced the probes: 13 collectives per step on two devices. The runner now sends only
  Hy/Hz to the right and Ey/Ez to the left, one packed transfer each way, and sums the probes
  once after the loop: 2 collectives per step.
- Time per step on two RTX 4090 nodes: 3.4–4.0× faster at 10.8 M cells and 2.7–3.4× faster
  at 324 M cells. Inside one node (three A6000) the gain is 6 %.
- Results are unchanged bit for bit on JAX 0.10.2 and 0.6.2. On JAX 0.4.33 (CPU) a model with
  PEC walls, dispersive blocks and a lumped port can move by 1–2 float32 ULP. Gradients
  through a traced source amplitude can move by 1–2 float32 ULP.
