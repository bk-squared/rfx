### Fixed — uniform-grid distributed runs return full results across JAX processes (#1202)

- When the device mesh spans more than one JAX process, the runner passes the
  material, dispersion, CPML and PEC-mask arrays to the jitted scan as explicit
  arguments instead of closing over them (the closure raised "Closing over
  jax.Array that spans non-addressable devices"), and replicates the final fields
  and probe trace onto every process with `process_allgather`. That gather returns
  host arrays, so a multi-process result is not differentiable through it.
- The multi-process path is selected by the devices in the mesh, not by
  `jax.process_count()`: a multi-process job that meshes only its own local
  devices keeps the single-process path, as before.
- Non-uniform grids (dx/dy/dz profiles) are refused with `NotImplementedError` when
  the mesh spans processes; their update bodies still close over the spacing arrays.
- Single-process runs keep their original scan and gather paths and their results
  are bit-identical (14 pinned SHA256 traces); a test pins that the scan body on
  one process still captures the material arrays by closure, and that it captures
  none on the multi-process path.
