### Added — `run(..., ringdown=RingdownSpec())`: a wire port's S-parameters from a record cut while the structure still rings (#1254)

- A resonator hit by a short pulse rings long after the pulse. A DFT of a record
  cut while it rings misses the part it has not reached, and every S-parameter
  built from it is wrong by that part. After the pulse the update is one fixed
  linear map, so the port voltage and current are sums of the same damped
  oscillations: `Simulation.run(..., ringdown=rfx.ringdown.RingdownSpec())`
  identifies them on the second half of the record (one matrix pencil over the
  port channels, after harminv's anti-alias decimation toward the run's
  `freq_max`) and adds each spectrum's unrecorded tail in closed form.
  `Result.ringdown.s_params` has the shape and bins of `Result.s_params`.
- `Result.ringdown.report` holds the window, the poles (frequency, Q, decay
  rate, |λ| per step) and the witnesses of the completed number: the two-window
  difference W2 (completions from [T/2, T] and [T/2, 0.9 T], bar 1e-3 of |S|),
  no kept growing pole other than one at zero frequency within rounding,
  max |S_kk| ≤ 1 + 1e-3, and every source below 1e-6 of its peak over the
  window. A failed witness warns.
- The port voltage and current come from read-only probes on the port's own
  cells, removed from every output. After the run they are accumulated the
  run's way and must equal the run's own port accumulators bit for bit, or
  `run()` raises; every other output is byte-identical to the same run without
  `ringdown=`.
- Measured on a 12 × 11 × 5 mm metal box filled with eps_r 2.2, TM110 at
  12.42 GHz with a loaded Q of about 230, on the uniform and the graded lane
  (`tests/unit/sparams/test_ringdown_run.py`): a 2.86 ns record's plain S11 is
  0.94 dB and 15.8° off the completed 30.5 ns answer, its completed S11
  2.4e-4 dB and 1.5e-3° off, with W2 = 2.4e-5.
- Scope: wire ports (`add_port(..., extent=...)`) and a record of fixed length;
  one port on the uniform lane, the driven columns on the graded lane. Lumped,
  microstrip, waveguide, coaxial and Floquet ports, TFSF, Kerr media,
  `devices=`, `until_decay=` and a source still on inside the window are
  refused with the reason.
