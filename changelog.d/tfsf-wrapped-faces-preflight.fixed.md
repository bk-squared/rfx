### Fixed — Preflight judges the side faces of a uniform-mesh TF/SF plane wave as the run solves them: periodic, with no absorber (#PR)

- On a uniform mesh, an open TF/SF plane wave makes the run solve its side faces as periodic (y and z, or z alone for an oblique method-B source): the absorber pads stay allocated, but the time step wraps them. Preflight read the declared absorber there instead. So a slab drawn across the whole side extent, as the plane wave needs, drew a "geometry in the absorber" advisory. A pole medium also drew "a pad without the pole". Neither holds: the pads are wrapped and carry the material, pole included. Both advisories are gone on those faces, and checks that read face depth or periodicity now read the run's own flags. The slab R/T tutorial's slab run loses its absorber advisory.
- Graded meshes keep their declared side absorbers, and a closed-box TF/SF source wraps no face; neither changes. No solver number changes: the time step already used these flags.
