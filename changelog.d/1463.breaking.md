### BREAKING — A port's waveform is its open-circuit source voltage on every mesh (#1463)

`add_port(waveform=w, impedance=R)` (lumped and wire) and `add_msl_port` now drive the port
as a voltage source w behind R: the open-circuit voltage across the port is w on any mesh.
S-parameters do not change; absolute fields, probe traces, flux power and far fields of
port-driven models do. Field ratio new/old (power: its square), with A⊥ the port edge's
transverse cell area and n the number of wire edges: uniform mesh n·d/(R·A⊥) — ×20 at 1 mm
and ×40 at 0.5 mm for a 50 Ω lumped port; graded mesh n·d/R — ×2e-5 at 1 mm and ×1e-5 at
0.5 mm. An MSL feed changes by its own load conductance. To keep earlier absolute values,
divide the waveform by the ratio.
