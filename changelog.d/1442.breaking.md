### BREAKING — `add_source(amplitude_kind=None)` is a current in amperes on every solver path (#1442)

`None` (the default) now means `'current'` (E += Cb·I/dV) on every path, as the
deprecation warning announced for 2.0. The absolute field of such a source changes;
S-parameters and ratios of probes do not. To keep earlier numbers pass the kind:
uniform `run()` with PEC, and ADI: `amplitude_kind='field'`; uniform `run()` with
CPML/UPML and uniform `forward()`: `amplitude_kind='current'` with the waveform
amplitude multiplied by the cell volume dV; graded meshes: unchanged. ADI now
accepts `'current'`. A float16 run whose source would overflow is refused before
the first step (it returned NaN).
