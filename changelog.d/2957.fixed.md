### Fixed — Ring-down completion and residue fit ran out of memory on long records (#2957)

`forward(ringdown=)` formed its completion arrays over the whole identification window (window samples × channels × padded poles), and the ring-down residue fit solved one window-long basis on the host. A long record therefore ran out of device memory after its field run, or its residue fit was refused at the 4 GiB identification bound. Both now run over balanced blocks of the record, each at least as long as the matrix it factors is wide, so their memory depends on the block size and the pole count, not on the record length. On a 24 GB card the forward-mode gradient of an 8 µs closed-box record (2.1 million samples, 8 channels, 512 padded poles) now completes: the completion adds 285 bytes per record sample (76 bytes in reverse mode). A residue fit whose memory estimate was 4.8–6.2 GB, and which was therefore refused, is now estimated at 88 MB. Results equal the unblocked fit to 3×10⁻¹¹ of the peak in double precision. In single precision they differ by no more than float32 solves in another sample order do, except the residue derivative of the shortest window tested (5×10⁻⁴ of its peak, inside the 10⁻² bar of the record-length gradient check).
