### Fixed — a lumped RLC element on an addressed edge of a switched `pinned_sheet_layout` sheet sat under the switched metal (#2962)

On a graded mesh, `forward(pinned_sheet_layout=...)` with a lumped RLC element (folded or solved) whose E edge is an addressed edge of a switched sheet now keeps that one edge free of the switched metal, as a design occupancy or design region does, and warns once (`Port occupancy release: lumped_rlc[i] ...`). Before, the scene was refused; before that refusal, a folded element at layout value 1 was shorted with no warning, its time series equal to the run without it. A concrete layout value 0 on that edge gives no warning. Runs with no element on an addressed edge are unchanged.
