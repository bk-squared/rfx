### Fixed — Conformal PEC cut cells at Box edges and corners take the exact Box overlap (#2895)

With `conformal_pec=True`, `conformal_pec="cut_cell_floor"` or `Boundary(conformal=True)`,
the open length and area of each cut cell along an axis-aligned PEC Box's edges and corners
now come from the exact overlap with the Box. Before, a linear rule on node distances was
wrong there by up to the whole offset: a PEC block in a closed cavity resonated as if its
faces sat 0.03 to 0.27 cell off their drawn planes. Two Boxes less than a cell apart now
leave their gap open. Cells that a sphere, a cylinder or a sheet continued into the absorber
also reaches keep that body's rule. Building the cell table takes longer with many Boxes:
8.6 s against 2.2 s for 160 random Boxes on 80 x 80 x 40 cells.
