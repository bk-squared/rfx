`sim.run(devices=[...])` now returns the full S-matrix for uniform models with
single-cell, excited lumped ports, including the default S-parameter request.
Explicit `s_param_freqs` and `s_param_n_steps` use the same conventions and
extractor as one-device runs. Each drive records five owning-cell field samples
per receive port: `n_steps × 5 × 4 bytes × n_ports` per device.
Extended and passive ports, specialized port families, and graded-mesh ports
retain their existing restrictions.
