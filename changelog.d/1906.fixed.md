### Fixed — forward(ringdown=) differentiates through a traced graded cell profile (#1906)

`forward(..., ringdown=RingdownSpec())` with a traced `dx_profile`/`dy_profile`/`dz_profile` (mesh as a design
variable) stopped with a ConcretizationTypeError while placing its port-channel probes, and then returned NaN because
the host pole identification read traced port weights. Probes now resolve on the nominal mesh positions use on a traced
axis, and the identification sizes the port V/I there (a different combination of the same recorded samples, so the same poles); residues and S stay
traced. On the traced-mesh patch board the traced S equals the concrete one to 3e-7 of its peak at a deformed profile,
and the gradient passes the early-start witness (3e-6, bar 1e-2). Concrete meshes are unchanged.
