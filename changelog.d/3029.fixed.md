### Fixed — the graded-node warning for a lossy thin conductor and the sheet-edge docstrings now say what rfx computes (#3029)

- The `thin_conductor_graded_node` preflight warning said a DC film is normalized by the occupied cell's width. A DC film and a surface-impedance sheet both spread their conductance over the node's dual spacing, so the folded sheet resistance is the declared one. The warning now says so.
- The `rfx.mesh_edges` docstring gave a free sheet's edge offset as 0.27 cell at a cell ratio of 1/2 and 0.60 at 2. The measured values are 0.28 and 0.56 to 0.57, as the support matrix already states; the 0.57 is extrapolated from two meshes, and the support matrix and the model-on-grid page now say so.
- The same docstring gave 0.37 cell for a strip three cells above a ground plane with square cells. That is a one-mesh value: the same gap meshed with 3, 6, 12 and 24 cells extrapolates to 0.352. Its 0.31 at cell ratios 1/2 and 1/4 are one-mesh values not re-measured, and the docstring now says so.
- No computed result, warning trigger or refusal changes.
