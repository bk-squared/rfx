### Fixed — the absorber's magnetic-field update now reads its loss profile at the magnetic field's own position (#1012)

- In an absorbing boundary (CPML) the conductivity, stretch and shift profiles are graded across
  the layers, and E and the transverse H it couples to sit half a cell apart on the Yee lattice.
  `init_cpml` built one profile per face, sampled at the E positions, and the H update read the same
  one, so the absorber the magnetic field saw was displaced by half a cell from the one it stood in.
  The observable is mirror symmetry: a symmetric source in a symmetric box must give mirror-image
  fields, which is a property of the coordinates and not an absorber-strength fit. Magnetic profiles
  are now sampled half a cell inward on low faces and half a cell outward before the high-face
  reversal; the E profiles are unchanged.
  At normal incidence the absorber's worst reflection over 0.5–15 GHz is
  −14.5 → −33.4 dB with 8 layers and −22.5 → −78.4 dB with 16 layers;
  a thin (4–6 layer) absorber inside a source's reactive near field keeps more
  low-frequency field with this change (#1272).
- Measured on one GPU with one comparator (VESSL 369367260765, receipts under
  `scripts/diagnostics/cpml_stagger_witness/gpu-369367260765/`): against a 5 % far-channel
  requirement the far-field `Hy` peak-relative residual is `0.139` before and `0.00039` after.
- **Every number that depends on an absorbing boundary moves.** The uniform and both distributed
  lanes receive the same profiles. A face with a single active layer keeps its original coefficient
  (there is no grading interval to stagger), and explicit legacy `CPMLParams` / `AxisParams` without
  magnetic profiles keep their old behaviour.
- Distributed nonuniform forward calls keep magnetic CPML cell spacings static alongside
  the electric spacings when profile arrays are passed into the compiled time loop.
  The uniform distributed initializer reuses its boundary spacing for both profiles.
- The distributed nonuniform E update reads the real high-x/high-y profiles, as
  its H update does. With an Ez source six cells from a PEC y-high wall, the
  two-device probe difference from one device falls from 1.009/0.160 to
  1.6e-6/6.7e-6 of the probe peaks at 3/12 cells from the wall over 600 steps
  (PEC faces of #1235; PMC faces and the uniform distributed lane remain open there).
- The Debye public-entry recovery test runs 100 optimizer iterations with its
  existing recovery tolerances (previously 25 iterations).
- Re-pinned the six WR-90 slab cells from VESSL 369367264100, retaining the
  12 thru/short cells from 369367264028 and checking the live record's SHA-256:
  slab distance from Airy at fine/mid/coarse is 0.0149/0.0607/0.287, previously
  0.101/0.232/0.581. The NTFF ladder now requires `|P_ntff/P_flux - 0.5| <= 0.005`
  on each rung, replacing measured centers 0.5003/0.5150/0.5226 with bands ±0.02;
  the unchanged base-rung band is ±0.003. The matched-load reflection gate is
  0.05 (previously 0.20), with a four-layer witness above 0.08 (previously 0.25)
  and absorbed fraction above 0.95 unchanged; measured reflection is
  0.0135/0.119 for 10/4 layers. The patch's negative test restores both the
  cut ground and integer-node magnetic grading through a test-side helper:
  settling is -24.4192 dB with both defects (VESSL 369367264159), versus
  -44.69 dB with continuation alone switched off (VESSL 369367264100).
  Its -40 dB and -2e-5 per-step decay thresholds are unchanged. CPU golden
  changes are max complex S11 difference 4.53986e-5 for no RLC and max complex
  S difference 4.81506e-4/3.35468e-3 for waveguide false/flux normalization;
  normalize=True regenerates within 5.6e-17 and retains its file and SHA.
  The nonuniform until-decay spectrum witness now uses 12 absorber layers
  (the other gates keep four): its in-band error against the 3x fixed run is
  0.000826, versus 0.0296 with four layers on #1012; main read 0.0018.
  The finite-flux two-gradings witness uses 16 layers (the other gates keep
  eight): at 9.4 GHz its cross-mesh difference is 0.0364 (0.0415 with eight
  layers on #1012; main read 0.0325), against the unchanged 0.0375 envelope;
  the empirical prefactor remains 3.0.
