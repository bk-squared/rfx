Far-field complex phase now uses the physical domain origin on uniform and
non-uniform meshes, including PEC/PMC faces with no padding and absorbers
thinner than the configured budget. NTFF boxes with conflicting grid padding
metadata are rejected; omitted metadata is resolved from the realized grid.
Radiation-pattern CSV `gain_dBi` now contains absolute IEEE gain instead of
peak-normalized power. Optional `input_power=` exports realized gain.
Gain without input power, directivity and efficiency now reject incomplete
sphere coverage instead of treating partial radiated power as total power.
A single azimuth cut retains the axisymmetric assumption; realized gain
with `input_power=` accepts partial patterns. NTFF margin errors are no longer
hidden by pad conflicts, and legacy unspecified scalar padding means zero.
