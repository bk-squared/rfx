### Fixed — a lumped inductor no longer carries a spurious series resistance ω²LΔt: a 2 nH on 1 mm cells read 9.5 Ω at 8 GHz (Q ≈ 10) and now reads lossless (#1245)

- `add_lumped_rlc` with an inductance in the parallel topology, and a pure L
  under either topology keyword, applied the inductor current I^{n+1} over the
  step n → n+1 (backward Euler, half a step late against the time-centred Yee
  update). Its impedance was jωL·sinc(ωΔt/2)·e^{−jωΔt}: a series resistance
  ≈ ω²LΔt, first order in the cell. Measured with a plane wave on a sheet of
  pure 2 nH elements: 0.61 Ω at 2 GHz and 9.5 Ω at 8 GHz on 1 mm cells, half
  that on 0.5 mm. A tank or matching network built from lumped L came out
  damped (resonance depth and bandwidth wrong; the resonant frequency right).
- The inductor is now solved together with its edge field in one trapezoidal
  step, time-centred like the field (as the series elements since #1163): it
  stores and returns energy without loss, and its reactance is right to second
  order (jωL·tan(ωΔt/2)/(ωΔt/2); +0.08 % at 8 GHz on 1 mm cells). The same
  sheet reads |Re Z| ≤ 1e-6 of ωL; an L‖C in a closed PEC box keeps its energy
  to 4e-15 (float64) over 10 periods, where the old update lost 92 %.
- Parallel R and C (folded into the edge) and series elements are unchanged,
  bit for bit. The inductor's parallel update now needs E^n, which both scan
  bodies already pass.
- Two lumped elements that each carry their own solve — a parallel element
  with L, or a series element with two or more components — on the same edge
  (after the positions snap to the mesh) are now refused with a message naming
  both declarations and the edge. Each solved against a field the other had
  already changed, and the pair gained energy (two series L + C: ×4 in 6400
  steps in a closed box, as before this change; two parallel inductors, which
  the old inductor's loss had hidden). Model them as one element with the
  combined value, or place them on different edges. Folded parallel R and C
  may still share the edge with one such element.
