### Fixed — a mesh split across devices refuses the cases it solved wrongly or crashed on, and runs a thin plate (#1350, #1351)

- `forward(distributed=True)` on a non-uniform mesh accepted a periodic axis and laid absorbers inside the domain, so its fields were
  1.3 to 2.3 times their peak off the one-device run. A periodic axis is now refused there, as it already was on `run(devices=N)`.
- A split whose boundary device holds fewer cells along the split axis than that face's absorber layers plus one exchanged cell lost
  the innermost absorber layer or crashed. It is now refused, with the face, the cell counts and the device count in the message.
  A plate between two walls thinner than the absorber depth no longer crashes, and a split with no absorber on x is no longer refused.
