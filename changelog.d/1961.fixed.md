PEC and surface-impedance thin conductors and pinned sheets now reject non-unit or traced `eps_r` at declaration instead of ignoring it.
Leave sheet `eps_r` at 1 and declare surrounding dielectric with `add(..., material=...)`; the sheet uses the permittivity of its cells.
A saved design document with such a sheet at `eps_r != 1` now fails to load with that error instead of loading with the value dropped.
