### Fixed — Ring-down completion and pinned sheets refuse traced inputs they mishandled silently (#1961)

- `forward(ringdown=...)`, `run(ringdown=...)` and `until_identified=True` skipped the source-off check for a drive
  whose waveform is traced (under `jax.grad`, `jax.jit` or `jax.vmap`), so a drive still at full strength inside the
  identification window completed with finite S. Such a drive is now refused by name: use concrete waveform values
  (S does not depend on the drive's amplitude), or pin `dt=` on a traced mesh.
- A gradient through the node indices of `add_pinned_sheet` (`plane_index`, `i_range`, `j_range`) was exactly zero
  with no warning. A traced index is now refused by name; to move a sheet continuously, trace the cell widths
  around a fixed index.
- The ring-down frequency refusal names the call's own entry and argument (`run(ringdown=...)` with `s_param_freqs`).
- Known limitations: a `forward(ringdown=...)` completion whose witnesses fail warns nothing; read its `report.ok`.
