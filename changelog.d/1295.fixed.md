### Fixed — a source, probe or port declared exactly midway between two nodes lands on the same node on the non-uniform lane as on the uniform lane (#1295)

- The uniform lane puts such a coordinate on the even node; the non-uniform
  lane took the lower one. On 1 mm cells a feed at y = 9.5 mm sat at 10 mm on
  one lane and 9 mm on the other. The non-uniform lane now takes the even node
  too, and on equal cells computes it as the uniform lane does, `round(x/dx)`.
- Non-uniform runs whose source, probe, port, DFT or flux plane, NTFF box or
  waveguide aperture edge sits exactly on a half node above an odd node move up
  one node. The node is now found in float64; the old float32 lookup could also
  pick the other node for a coordinate within float32 roundoff of a half node
  (up to 2.4e-7 m on a 240 mm axis). Other coordinates, and the uniform lane,
  do not change.
