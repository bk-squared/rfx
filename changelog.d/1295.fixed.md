### Fixed — a source, probe or port declared exactly midway between two nodes lands on the same node on the non-uniform lane as on the uniform lane (#1295)

- Both lanes now put such a coordinate on the even node; the non-uniform lane
  took the lower one, so on 1 mm cells a feed at y = 9.5 mm sat at 9 mm there
  and at 10 mm on the uniform lane. Non-uniform runs with a feature on such a
  half node move one node up; the node is also found in float64 now, which
  moves coordinates within float32 roundoff of a half node. Others do not move.
- A wire vertex or PEC sheet at a half node still goes to the lower node
  (#1342). A port, source or probe declared at the same coordinate would land
  one cell off it; preflight (`half_node_split`) and `run()`/`forward()` now
  refuse that on both lanes, with the reason.
