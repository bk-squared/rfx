### Fixed — a probe that holds only rounding no longer decides the ring-down settling verdict (#1909)

- A probe at a symmetry null (for example Hz over a vertical source above a ground plane) holds only rounding,
  at about 10⁻⁴ of the largest record in float32. It used to score −33 to +2.5 dB, so `run()` and `forward()` warned
  that every DFT quantity integrates a cut transient while the physical probes read below −130 dB.
- Such a record is now skipped when its peak is below 10⁴ × eps of the largest record of the same quantity (E, H,
  V or I). It is named in `settling_witness["skipped_records"]` with its ratio and reason; if every record were
  skipped the verdict is undetermined, never a pass. float16 records (`precision="mixed"`) are scored as before.
- S-parameters and fields are unchanged.
