### Changed — one DFT and one time stamp for every stored spectrum (#PR)

- Every record is stamped with the time its field holds: after step n, E at
  (n+1)·dt and H at (n+½)·dt. Spectra that were stamped n·dt (port V and I,
  and DFT planes and flux on a graded mesh) turn by exp(−jω·dt) in absolute
  phase. S, Z, wave amplitudes, flux, gain and |E| do not change.
- DFT planes, flux and ports share one kernel whose phase error no longer grows
  with the record length (it reached 5.7e-3 rad at 1e5 steps).
- A `precision="float64"` run now weights the port DFT in complex128; its S
  moves by up to 6e-5 of peak, only at bins driven far below the band peak.
