### Changed — Shared physical-clock DFT for measurement records (#PR)

Plane, flux and port spectra now use one DFT kernel with physical leapfrog timestamps and bounded-error split phase tables. Stored-record replay uses the same kernel. Existing sample sets are retained, including the waveguide's historical final-sample exclusion; ratios and magnitudes retain their existing convention while previously early-stamped spectra acquire the physical absolute phase.
