### Added — a periodic axis on the graded mesh, in run() and forward() on one device (#1221)

- `BoundarySpec(x="periodic", ...)` on a graded mesh now runs instead of being
  refused: N cells span exactly the declared period and the fields wrap across
  it as on the uniform mesh. A 24 mm ring at 1 mm cells resonates at
  12.466451 GHz against the Yee value 12.466452 GHz, and a graded run matches
  the uniform run of the same request.
- A periodic axis keeps a constant cell size. Refused with a message on the
  graded mesh: a graded profile along a periodic axis, a plane wave (TF/SF) or
  `interface_eps='dual_average'` together with a periodic axis, and Bloch
  phases. Multi-device, subgridded and ADI runs keep refusing periodic axes.
