### Fixed — `forward()`, multi-device runs, waveguide-port runs and oblique RCS now back every absorbing face with a conducting plane, as `run()` does (#1549)

- Behind each absorbing (CPML or UPML) face `run()` sets tangential E to zero on the outermost node plane. `forward()`,
  `run(devices=...)`, the waveguide S-matrix entries and the oblique `compute_rcs` run left it out, so they solved a
  different boundary than `run()`. A conductor crossing an absorber in such a run could grow without bound (the issue's
  coax model reached about 3,800 times its pulse peak); `forward()` and `run()` now give the same record.
- Open-domain results without such a conductor move little: about 1e-5 of the field peak in a small open box, at most
  2e-6 in waveguide |S|, nothing measurable in the oblique RCS. Faces a feature wraps (TF/SF, Floquet) and PMC faces get
  no plane.
