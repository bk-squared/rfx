### Fixed — graded multi-device forward and run fall back to one device (#9999)

- `forward(distributed=True)` on a graded mesh with a waveguide port or TF/SF source
  now runs on one device with a warning, as `run()` does, instead of refusing.
- `run(devices=...)` on a graded mesh with a TF/SF source does the same instead of
  raising `ValueError`.
