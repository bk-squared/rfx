### Fixed — a far-field box face one cell inside the plane-wave injection plane is refused instead of mixing the two field regions (#9999)

- A total-field/scattered-field plane wave is injected between two x planes:
  inside them the grid holds incident + scattered field, outside it holds
  scattered only. With the second-order face-centre layout an x face of the
  Huygens box reads E on the face node and the two half-cell magnetic-field
  planes that straddle it, so a face at `x_hi + 1` took its E from the
  scattered-field side and one magnetic-field plane from the total-field side.
  The whole incident H then entered a face that should carry scattered field
  only. On an 18 mm eps_r = 4 cube at 9/10/11 GHz with dx = 3 mm the band
  backscatter came out 1.4405e-01 m^2 instead of 1.2049e-03 m^2 — about 21 dB
  too high — from a run that finished normally with no warning. `compute_rcs`
  places its own box clear of this; a box built by hand and passed to
  `run(..., tfsf=..., ntff=...)` was never checked.
- Both the uniform and the non-uniform runner now refuse such a box before the
  time loop, naming the face, its index, the sample that falls on the other
  side and the index to move to.
