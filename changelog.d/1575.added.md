### Added — preflight reports the expected error of a low-resistance thin film (#1575)

Preflight now warns when a lossy thin film's two-plane realization is expected to transmit too little and absorb too much at `freq_max`. It reports the estimated errors and a cell-size remedy when `(k0 dx)(eta0/Rs)` exceeds 3, using the occupied cell width on uniform and graded meshes. The warning also appears before `run()` and `forward()`; simulation results are unchanged.
