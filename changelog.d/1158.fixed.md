### Fixed — `rfx.material_fit.eval_lorentz` returned a constant ε∞ for a Drude pole (#1158)

- A pole built with `drude_pole(omega_p, gamma)` has `omega_0 == 0`; `eval_lorentz`
  recovered `delta_eps = kappa / omega_0**2`, set it to zero there, and so returned
  a dispersionless `eps_inf` (off by up to 2.3 for ε∞ 2.7, plasma 10 GHz, collision
  rate 4 GHz over 6–14 GHz). It now evaluates
  `kappa / (omega_0**2 - w**2 + 2j*delta*w)` from the pole fields: identical for
  `omega_0 > 0`, and `eps_inf - omega_p**2 / (w**2 - 1j*gamma*w)` for a Drude pole.
- No simulated result changes. The FDTD update reads the pole directly; a Drude
  slab's reflection matches the transfer-matrix closed form (mean |ΔR| 0.0060 over
  6–14 GHz), which a new analytic test pins. Only the permittivity you evaluate or
  plot with `eval_lorentz` was wrong.
- New analytic tests: a Drude slab's R(f), the slab's realized thickness for all
  dispersive slab tests, and a PEC cavity on a z mesh with two fine bands
  (TM111 within 0.024 % of the closed form of the realized depth).
