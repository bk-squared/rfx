### Fixed — a graded model whose dielectric stops short of a padded face is refused (#1511)

- On graded meshes (single and multi-device), a dielectric drawn out to a
  domain face whose rasterized mask stops short of it now raises
  `PadFillShortfall`, as on uniform meshes, instead of being solved with
  vacuum in the absorber pad. Preflight, `realized_geometry()` and
  fidelity reports list the shortfall without raising.
