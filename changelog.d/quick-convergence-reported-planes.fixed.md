### Fixed — `quick_convergence` moved a waveguide port's reported probe plane when it refined the mesh (#PR)

`quick_convergence` kept `probe_offset` and `ref_offset` as cell counts on every rung, so at a dx factor f the reported probe plane moved toward the source by `probe_offset·dx·(1 − f)` and the reported S21 phase moved by β times that length while the structure stayed the same. On an empty WR-90 guide with the default `probe_offset=10`, refining by 0.5 and 0.25 shifted the S21 phase by 0.66–1.41 rad and 0.98–2.11 rad over 8.2–12.4 GHz. Each rung now reports S21 between the factor-one model's planes in metres, and the waveguide offsets and a lumped port's `reference_plane_cells` scale with the factor (rounded half up, at least 1). Two offsets that would round to the same count are refused. Results at factor 1 are unchanged.
