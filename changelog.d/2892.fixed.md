### Fixed — 2-D ports on a conductor that reaches the absorber stopped with an IndexError; they now get the same verdict as their 3-D extrusion (#2892)

A 2-D model (`2d_tez` or `2d_tmz`) with a CPML absorber and a lumped port on a conductor that reaches the absorber stopped in `run()` and `forward()` with an `IndexError`.
A TEz port across two plates now warns that both continue into the x-lo, x-hi absorber, at any declared z.
A TMz port beside a strip gives no termination warning, as in its 3-D extrusion.
