### BREAKING — Conformal walls and permeability requests are checked before stepping (#1299, #1362)

- Topology optimization, mixed-port S-matrices and batched material sweeps refuse
  conformal wall declarations; use staircase walls or uniform `run()` for fields.
- ADI `forward()` refuses `mu_r_override`; UPML refuses `cpml_kappa_max != 1`.
- Lumped/wire S-matrices refuse `conformal_pec=True`; explicit `False` requests
  staircase PEC on every run lane, including a conformal declaration.
- Direct `run_uniform()` reads the conformal declaration by default, and device
  fallbacks preserve an explicit `conformal_pec=False`.
