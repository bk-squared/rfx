### Fixed — a Debye or Lorentz material anywhere in a model no longer puts the whole grid back on the one-coefficient-per-cell E update (#1260)

- The dispersive E update now takes ε∞, σ and each pole's Δε per E component as the mean over the edge's four cells, like the plain update since #1210. A half-filled 24 mm PEC cube read its TE(1,0)-to-z mode +1.7 % high at dx = 1 mm once any pole was present (a 1 mm, Δε = 1e-6 Debye cube in a far corner was enough); it now reads the no-pole 5.2162 GHz, and a Debye or Lorentz filling converges at second order to the complex-ε transverse resonance (−3.2e-4 at 1 mm).
- A lumped port's load or a folded R/C in a dispersive model loads only its own E edge (#1236 on the dispersive lanes); the `UserWarning` that said otherwise is gone.
- On the distributed lanes the combined Debye + Lorentz update uses the single-device coefficients (its Lorentz term was scaled by 1/γ_Lorentz instead of 1/(γ_Lorentz + Σβ)).
- `DebyeCoeffs.ca/cb/cc/beta` and `LorentzCoeffs.ca/cb/cc/c` are now `(x, y, z)` tuples of arrays; `alpha`, `a`, `b` stay one array per pole. `init_debye` / `init_lorentz` take `periodic=`.
