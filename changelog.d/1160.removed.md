### Removed — six closed-form cross-validation cases and the weekly external-solver CI job (#1160)

- `04_multilayer_fresnel`, `22_dispersive_slab_fresnel`, `23_lossy_slab_fresnel`,
  `26_oblique_slab_fresnel`, `24_nu_rect_cavity_pozar` and `17_dielectric_sphere_mie`
  are gone with their committed records (28 MiB). Slab reflection (lossless, Debye,
  Lorentz, Drude, lossy; oblique TE at 30 and 45 degrees) and the graded-mesh
  cavity stay compared with their closed forms by the analytic tests under
  `tests/oracle/` (`docs/design_notes/20260921_crossval_role_redesign.md`).
- Not compared with a closed form by anything: oblique incidence beyond 45 degrees,
  and the monostatic RCS of a dielectric sphere. The removed sphere case recorded
  itself as unconverged (its RCS moves 11.7 dB at ka 1.75 and 29 dB at ka 2.5 when
  only the clearance to the domain walls changes); the same behaviour on a PEC
  sphere is tracked as issue 820. Treat a monostatic RCS from `compute_rcs` as
  sensitive to where the domain walls sit until that is understood.
- CI no longer runs an external solver: the weekly `crossval-external` Meep job is
  removed from `validation.yml`.
- Under `rfx/` one module docstring changes. Everything removed is at commit `b4cf8f29`.
