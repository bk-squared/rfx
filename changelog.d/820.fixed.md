### Fixed — normal-incidence `compute_rcs` reported every σ about 1.1 dB low; it now divides by the incident field the grid actually carries (#820)

- A radar cross section is the scattered far-field power over the incident power
  at the target. At normal incidence `compute_rcs` took the incident from the
  source waveform itself, but the plane-wave line adds that waveform at one node
  (a soft source) and launches only 1/(2 S cos(k̃Δx/2)) of it, S = cΔt/Δx:
  0.877 in field amplitude (−1.14 dB) at 40 cells per wavelength and the default
  Courant number, 0.92 (−0.72 dB) at 10. σ scales as |E_scat/E_inc|², so every
  normal-incidence σ — monostatic and the whole bistatic pattern — was low by the
  same number of dB.
- σ is now normalized by a replay of the same auxiliary line, recorded where it
  meets the total-field region (`rfx.sources.tfsf.measure_normal_incident_spectrum`),
  as the oblique path already was (#471). A PEC sphere at ka = 1, λ/40, moves
  from 0.40 dB below the exact Mie value to 0.73 dB above it; ratios between σ
  values, pattern shapes and optimizations on σ are unchanged.
- `compute_rcs_jax` takes the incident amplitude from the caller: pass
  `measure_normal_incident_spectrum(cfg, state, n_steps, freqs, dt)` on the same
  `init_tfsf` config, not the waveform spectrum.
