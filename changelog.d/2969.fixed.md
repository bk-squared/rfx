### Fixed — a drawn PEC sheet on a traced graded mesh was refused even when its normal axis was not traced (#2969)

On a graded mesh with traced cell widths and declared `nominal_profiles`, a drawn zero-thickness PEC sheet (a Box with one zero extent, or a PEC `add_thin_conductor`) whose normal axis is concrete now resolves once on the nominal mesh and then stays on those nodes as the traced widths move them, exactly like `add_pinned_sheet` on the same node ranges: fields and their derivatives with respect to the widths are equal bit for bit. Before, it was refused. A traced normal axis, and a traced in-plane axis without `nominal_profiles`, stay refused, with the axis named. Sheet sizes on a traced mesh are now judged on the nominal mesh by the same rule as a concrete build, including the 1 % size refusal.
