Graded meshes can now use `Simulation.run(devices=...)` with CPML boundaries,
Debye or Lorentz materials, and PEC volume geometry through the distributed
nonuniform runner shared with `forward(distributed=True)`. Runs retain probe
records, gathered final fields, grid metadata and settling witnesses.
Lumped/wire ports, NTFF and other unsupported graded distributed inputs remain
explicitly refused. Uniform distributed runs retain their existing runner.
