### Fixed — a waveguide port inside a refined band injects at its own cell, not at the boundary cell (#810)

- A waveguide port drives the guide through a one-sided TFSF boundary: an H
  correction on the upstream half-cell and an E correction on the source
  plane. Both divided by the grid's BOUNDARY cell. A port placed inside a
  refined band therefore injected at the boundary cell's scale: on a WR-90
  guide meshed 1.5 mm at the walls and 0.75 mm through the middle, both
  corrections ran at 0.5000 of what the local cell asks for (gap G13).
- Each half now reads the plane it acts on through the grid interface: the
  primal cell width where the H correction differences two E nodes, and the
  node's dual spacing where the E correction sits.
- Nothing moves on a uniform mesh, or wherever a port sits in a
  boundary-sized cell, because the local cell is the boundary cell there.
  That covers every non-uniform port fixture in the suite, which is why none
  of them caught this.
