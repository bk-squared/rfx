`Simulation.forward(pinned_sheet_layout=...)` switches the metal of declared pinned sheets edge by edge on the graded
(non-uniform) single-device lane: per `add_pinned_sheet` call, None or two {0, 1} arrays over the sheet's edges, passed as
an operand of the compiled solve, so one compile serves many metal layouts. Each layout gives byte-identical probe records
and S11 to the same metal drawn with `add_pinned_sheet`. Values strictly between 0 and 1 are refused, and differentiating
with respect to the layout raises a TypeError: it is a layout switch, not a sensitivity. Refused instead of approximated:
the uniform, distributed, ADI and subgridded lanes; a port, source, solved lumped RLC element, surface-impedance sheet, MSL
feed or current-moment monitor on a switched edge; a switched sheet that touches another sheet without overlapping it by
one node; a one-cell normal axis. `ForwardResult.sheet_reports_skipped` names the sheet reports not run on switched sheets.
