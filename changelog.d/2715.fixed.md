### Fixed — an S calculator measures the column of a port declared passive, where earlier versions refused a waveguide port at amplitude 0 or returned a zero column for an MSL port with excite=False (#2715)

- A waveguide port with amplitude 0.0 is driven at 1.0 for its own column; its S equals the matrix with every port
  driven, bit for bit.
- An MSL port with excite=False is excited for its own column with its own waveform or the calculator default.
- Nonzero declared amplitudes and the run()/forward() drive declarations are unchanged.
