### Changed — a TF/SF plane wave along a graded axis is refused instead of run with a warning (#2094)

- A plane wave whose propagation axis (x) has cells of more than one size now raises on `run()`,
  `forward()` and with `skip_preflight=True`; preflight reports it as an error.
- Before, it ran: the incident wave was built on the boundary cell while the grid carried it through
  the graded cells, and with no scatterer 0.72 of the peak leaked into the scattered-field region.
- Grading a transverse axis (y or z) is unchanged and supported. A graded incident line is planned (#2078).
