### Changed — a dielectric Sphere or Cylinder too small to hold a cell is refused at every position (#PR)

- A material Sphere whose radius is below half the diagonal of the cell that contains its centre (0.866 of the cell on a uniform mesh), or a Cylinder whose radius is below half the diagonal of that cell's cross-section (0.707 of the cell), is now refused wherever it is placed. Before, such a body ran at some positions and was refused as "ZERO cells" at others, so a position sweep or a position gradient could cross from a result to a refusal. Bodies in this size range were measured 1.15–6.4× over-polarized (the cavity-mode shift they cause, against the converged shift).
- The rule applies wherever an empty material volume is refused, including CSG leaves (a small Sphere used as a hole in `difference()`) and both passes of a refinement region. A 2D grid, a body centred outside the grid, and a traced centre or mesh keep the empty-cell rule; a traced radius is checked inside compiled code.
- `preflight()` warns ("Small dielectric body:") for an admitted dielectric Sphere, or a Cylinder across its radius, whose diameter is under three cells: a sphere two cells across measured 1.30× (1.25× without subpixel smoothing) and a rod two cells across 1.10× (1.13×); four cells read 1.07×.
