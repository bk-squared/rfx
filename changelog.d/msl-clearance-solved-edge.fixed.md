### Fixed — The microstrip clearance advisories measure from the solved strip edge and no longer grow with the absorber depth (#PR)

- Before, the lateral and feed advisories added the absorber depth (layers × cell) to a 2 h_sub margin and stated a Z0 bias of up to 15 %. A deeper absorber therefore raised more advisories: on a line board with 14 cells of lateral clearance and a 2 mm feed, the lateral advisory fired from 16 layers (the default) up, the feed advisory from 24, and neither at 8.
- Now the lateral advisory reads the distance from the strip's solved edge to the absorber's inner face and warns below 0.733 h_sub (2.2 cells at 3 cells per h_sub). The feed advisory warns below 1.33 h_sub (4 cells). On other meshes the thresholds are at least 2.2 and 4 absorber end cells, and the text says that mesh was not measured. No Z0 bias figure is stated.
- These thresholds come from one line board at 3 cells per h_sub with 8 to 32 layers. At 2.2 cells, S with 8 layers differs from S with 32 by 2.8×10⁻³, falling to 1.9×10⁻⁴ at 36 cells; with 16 layers the difference is at most 6.1×10⁻⁵. Meeting a threshold does not make S passive with 8 layers (see the known limitations). PEC and PMC walls, and faces with no active absorber, keep 2 h_sub, now read from the solved edge: a strip drawn exactly 2 h_sub from such a face now warns.
