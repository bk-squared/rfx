### Added — Complete field-probe spectra without wire ports (#2759)

- `RingdownSpec(probe_freqs=..., identification_probes=...)` completes a
  soft-source model's probe spectra on each field kind's physical DFT clock.
- `run()` returns `ringdown.probe_spectra`; `forward()` also provides the
  differentiable early-window twin `probe_spectra_long` and relative witnesses.
- Port S and probe completion require separate calls. Probe completion does
  not form passivity or port-accumulator witnesses and does not support early stop.
