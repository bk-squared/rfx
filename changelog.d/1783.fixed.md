### Fixed — one step-count rule on every path: graded run() and forward() no longer differ by one step (#1783)

At a whole-number boundary, graded `run()` and `forward()` took step counts one apart.
Preflight, ADI, waveguide S-parameters and the openEMS export each had their own copy of the rule.
All paths now use `rfx.stepping.step_count.steps_for_periods`; uniform counts are unchanged.
It replaces `NonUniformGrid.steps_for_periods`, added in #1774 and unreleased.
