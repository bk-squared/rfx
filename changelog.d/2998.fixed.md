### Fixed — material-fit probe time base (#2998)

- Under `normalization="per_probe_max"`, the fit proxy and loss phase no longer
  carry +2πf·dt for E probes or +πf·dt for H probes. For E probes on 1 mm cells,
  this was 6.9° at 10 GHz and 20.6° at 30 GHz, biasing fitted poles against
  targets on rfx's time base. Magnitudes and `reference_probe` with probes of
  one kind are unchanged.
