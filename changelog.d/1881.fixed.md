### Fixed — Graded current drive rounds the same eager and under jax.jit (#1881)

On one device, a graded `forward(eps_override=...)` wrapped in `jax.jit` drove a current source inside a dispersive
(Debye/Lorentz) medium 1-2 float32 ULP differently from the same call run eagerly. The drive is now formed as
`Cb * (1/dV) * w` on the single- and multi-device graded paths alike, the product XLA compiles, so both agree bit for bit.
With a dispersive medium at the source, eager runs move by a few float32 ULP (now equal to jit and to
multi-device); runs without one are unchanged. Refs #1856.
