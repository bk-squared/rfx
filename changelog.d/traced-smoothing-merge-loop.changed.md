### Changed — A shape-coordinate gradient under subpixel smoothing builds its permittivity about four times faster with periodic axes (#PR)

With `subpixel_smoothing=True` (or the dielectric stage of `"kottke_pec"`) and a traced shape coordinate, the smoothed permittivity is built on the traced path, which merges the medium pieces along each sample's normal over every shape and periodic image. That merge was compiled as an unrolled chain whose cost grew with the square of the piece count; it now runs as a compiled loop. The built permittivity and its derivatives are unchanged bit for bit. One εr 4 Sphere in a 57×48×48-cell domain with y and z periodic: a traced build takes 2.00 s instead of 8.20 s, compiles in 12.4 s instead of 25.8 s, and peaks at 1.61 GiB instead of 2.11 GiB; with four Spheres, 26 s instead of 142 s and 9.9 GiB instead of 17.2 GiB. Builds without a traced coordinate are unchanged.
