### Changed — A traced subpixel-smoothing build with two periodic axes is about four times faster (#PR)

With `subpixel_smoothing=True` (or the dielectric stage of `"kottke_pec"`) and a traced input to the permittivity build (a shape coordinate, a material value or a cell size, as under `jax.grad` or `jax.jit`), the build merges the medium pieces along each sample's normal over every shape and periodic image. That merge was compiled as an unrolled chain whose cost grew with the square of the piece count; it now runs as a compiled loop. The built permittivity and its derivatives are unchanged bit for bit. One εr 4 Sphere in a 57×48×48-cell domain with y and z periodic: a traced build takes 2.00 s instead of 8.20 s, compiles in 12.4 s instead of 25.8 s, and peaks at 1.61 GiB instead of 2.11 GiB; with four Spheres, 26 s instead of 142 s and 9.9 GiB instead of 17.2 GiB. With one periodic axis the same Sphere's traced build is slower, 0.62 s instead of 0.40 s, while it compiles in 3.5 s instead of 7.3 s; without periodic axes it is unchanged (0.10 s). Untraced builds are unchanged.
