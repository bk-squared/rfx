### Fixed — surface-impedance sheets on multi-device runs
`add_thin_conductor(..., surface_impedance_f0=...)` sheets now run with
`run(devices=...)` and `forward(distributed=True)` on uniform and graded meshes
instead of being refused. Each slab applies the one-device sheet update, also for
a sheet on a slab boundary. With a lumped port on three CPU devices the fields,
S-parameters and probe records equal one device bit for bit; with soft point
sources the probe spectra are bit-identical on x86 CPUs and differ by at most
1.3e-5 of the largest bin on arm64 CPUs.
A sheet with a TF/SF source or `exchange_interval` other than 1 on a uniform
mesh, or with PMC faces on a graded mesh, is still refused before the first step.
The lumped/wire S scan and the uniform waveguide S calculator silently stepped a
sheet with a Debye or Lorentz medium (the sheet update replaced the pole update at its edges) and now refuse, like `run()` and `forward()`, on one device or several; a sheet fully covered by PEC with such a medium is now refused on every entry too (a wider refusal, no model change).
