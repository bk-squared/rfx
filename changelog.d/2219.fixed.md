### Fixed — surface-impedance sheets on multi-device runs (#2219)
`add_thin_conductor(..., surface_impedance_f0=...)` sheets now run with
`run(devices=...)` and `forward(distributed=True)` on uniform and graded meshes
instead of being refused; each slab applies the one-device sheet update, also on
a slab boundary. With a lumped port on three CPU devices fields, S-parameters and
probes equal one device bit for bit; point-source probe spectra are bit-identical
on x86 CPUs and within 1.3e-5 of the largest bin on arm64 CPUs. Still refused
before the first step: a sheet with TF/SF or `exchange_interval` != 1 (uniform),
with PMC faces (graded legacy step), with a graded `pec_occupancy_override`, or
with a Debye or Lorentz medium (also under PEC) on every entry, now including the
lumped/wire S scan and the uniform waveguide S, which silently stepped a different
model (the sheet update replaced the pole update).
