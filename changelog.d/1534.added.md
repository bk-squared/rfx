### Added — Structured microstrip diagnostics (#1534)

- Results from `run()`, `forward()`, and microstrip S-matrix calculators carry
  structured diagnostic records; refusals expose them on the exception.
- Microstrip preflight findings provide stable codes and named SI values while
  preserving existing messages and legacy issue filters. The immutable record
  is available as `rfx.Diagnostic`; unmoved families carry legacy codes.
