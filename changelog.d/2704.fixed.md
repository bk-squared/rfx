### Fixed — Ring-down identification refused short many-channel windows that a smaller decimation fits (#2704)

`rfx.ringdown.identify`, `early_start_witness` and `run(ringdown=)` refused a short window with many channels at a fine time step (`IdentificationMemoryRefused`). The cause was the decimation plan, which gave up when its largest first stage left too few samples. Such a window is now identified on the largest decimation that keeps the pencil's pole capacity and fits the memory bound. For example, a 72-channel, 1400-sample window at a 20 ps step and 600 MHz needed 4.47 GiB undecimated and now takes one stage of 8. Every window that was identified before keeps its plan and its results.
