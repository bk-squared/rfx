# Traced far-field precision
With `precision='float64'` and x64 on, the traced far-field transform now computes in float64.
Under `precision='float64'`, a requested frequency is carried float32-rounded (relative at most 6x10^-8, under 1 kHz at 10 GHz), and results are consistent at that frequency.
Float32 results are unchanged except on graded meshes with x64 on, which now return complex64 as requested (float32-rounding level).
