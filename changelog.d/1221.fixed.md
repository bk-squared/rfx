### Fixed — a periodic axis the user declares repeats every L, not L + dx (#1221 B2)

- A declared periodic axis kept the extra fence-post node meant for walls, so it repeated every L + dx. A 24 mm periodic ring resonated
  4.2 % low at dx = 1 mm, and a 10 mm `RISUnitCell` was a 10.5 mm cell. It now has L/dx cells: the ring is within 0.19 % of c/L at 1 mm
  and converges at second order, and the RIS cell is 10 mm. Applies to `BoundarySpec` periodic faces, Floquet at 0° and `RISUnitCell`.
- A point at L maps to 0. An interval may end at L but not cross it. A sheet or plane at L is the plane at 0. A coordinate outside the
  period is refused as before. An explicit dx that does not divide L is refused with the nearest dividing dx; an automatic dx is snapped
  down to divide L, with a warning.
- Unchanged: the transverse wrap a TF/SF source imposes without a declared periodic axis, 2-D modes, and every non-periodic model.
