### Fixed — `forward()` on a closed PEC box applies the walls; it had been solving an open box while `run()` solved the cavity (#1193)

- `forward()` derived its PEC axes as "the axes without CPML", and a
  `boundary="pec"` grid (no absorber, `cpml_layers == 0`) still reports
  `cpml_axes == "xyz"`, so the scan received `pec_axes == ""` and `apply_pec`
  never ran (the same holds for any absorber-free `BoundarySpec`, where the
  per-face masks already carried the walls, so those are unchanged). A 24 mm
  cube read 5.091 GHz through `forward()` against the
  analytic and `run()` TM110 8.831 GHz. With no absorber and a `pec`
  boundary the walls are now every non-periodic axis, as `run()` defaults.
  Pinned by `tests/unit/autodiff/test_forward_closed_box_walls.py` (the two
  entry points agree on the resonance and it is the cavity's).
- Any gradient taken through `forward()` on a closed cavity before this was
  the gradient of an open box. Comparisons between two `forward()`
  formulations were unaffected; comparisons against `run()` or an analytic
  cavity were not.
