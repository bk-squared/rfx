### Changed — the default pulse starts at zero and leaves no static charge (#9995)

- `GaussianPulse`, the drive every source and port uses when it is given no waveform, now
  starts from zero without a jump and its time integral is exactly zero, at every `cutoff`
  and with the same peak time `t0`. A soft source driven by it no longer leaves a static
  field behind after the pulse.
- Time records change only near the start, by at most 1.63e-3 of the pulse amplitude
  within the first `tau`; spectra change by the same small amount, at low frequency.

Upgrading to 2.0: the default pulse no longer leaves a static field, and time records change at the 1.6e-3 level near the start.
