### Fixed — Plate-fed coaxial ports no longer get a false pin-short advisory (#3031)

A coaxial port fed through a hole in a ground plate no longer gets the false
pin-short advisory. At 1 mm spacing, the check counted the plate's metal on all
12 nodes of the pin's first ring; the run clears that metal from the bore.

`coaxial_port_junction_short` is retired. Unnamed metal at the reference plane
is cleared from the bore or refused by name. A contact named in `terminates=`
that also reaches the outer conductor is a declared short at the reference
plane, the shorted-line standard. The refusal for unnamed front metal now says
so.
