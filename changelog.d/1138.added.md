### Added — mesh lines that follow conductor sheet edges: `rfx.mesh_edges.edge_aware_profile` (#1138)

- A PEC sheet is solved as if it ended about 0.35 cell beyond its last PEC node
  (measured 0.345 … 0.354 cell, both polarizations, two cell sizes, against a
  reference extrapolated at its measured first order; a flat PEC wall has no
  such offset). `edge_aware_profile` builds a `dx_profile` / `dy_profile` with
  a node 0.35 cell INSIDE every sheet edge and a node ON every face — the
  measured counterpart of the openEMS thirds rule (1/3).
- On the 2-D fin it was measured on, the frequency error against the fine-mesh
  reference over 17 drawn lengths goes from rms 1.14 % (max 2.29 %) on the
  uniform 1 mm grid to rms 0.08 % (max 0.15 %); a uniform grid with a SMALLER
  cell (0.5 mm) still reads rms 0.54 %. A 3-D patch's three strongest modes
  move 0.08 / 0.07 / 0.00 % when the in-plane cell is halved, against
  0.75 / 2.14 / 1.10 % on the uniform grid.
- Scope of the number: for a strip three cells above a ground wall it reads
  0.31 … 0.37 as the cell normal to the sheet goes from 1/4 of the in-plane
  cell to equal; a free-standing sheet with strongly non-square cells moves
  more (0.27 … 0.60) and takes `edge_offset=`.
- `edge_aware_profiles(domain, dx, sheets=[...], solids=[...])` builds the
  profiles straight from the Boxes; the sheet is then drawn at its true size.
  A seam between two abutting sheets is not an edge; a declared face on a sheet
  edge warns. The smallest cell stays at or above 0.75 of the target.
- New preflight advisory `sheet_effective_size`: a conductor sheet solved more
  than 1 % off its drawn size is reported in input units (drawn / nodes cover /
  solved as), including a sheet drawn exactly on the lattice, which is 0.7 cell
  long. 12 audited examples carry it today.
- The RT5880 patch crossval case on these lines, both arms at 70 source
  periods: ring-down resonance 2.4242 → 2.3904 GHz (−1.39 %; the advisory
  reports the uniform patch 1.38 % short), S11 dip 2.420 → 2.390 GHz and the −10 dB
  band 2.39–2.46 → 2.36–2.42 GHz, against the committed openEMS leg's dip at
  2.330 GHz (band 2.30–2.36).
- `fidelity_report()` no longer tells the user to put a mesh node ON a sheet's
  edge; for sheets its remedy now names this function.
