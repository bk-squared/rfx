# Where the frozen references for the MSL open-stub notch filter come from

Facts only.

## Which file describes which board

The substrate, the trace and the stub are the same everywhere: lossless
εr = 3.66, h_sub = 0.254 mm, trace and stub 0.6 mm wide, stub 12 mm. Only the
length of the line either side of the stub differs, and with it the box.

| record | line each side of the stub centre | box |
|---|---|---|
| `openems_tutorial.json` | 50 mm (the openEMS tutorial's own `MSL_length`) | 100 × 30 × 3 mm |
| `palace_fem.json` | 2.5 mm (5.0 mm port plane to port plane, port margin 1.0 mm) | 7.0 × 16.232 × 1.754 mm, trace centre y = 1.208 mm, stub centre x = 3.5 mm |
| the case's rfx runs (`test_msl_notch_filter.py`) | 10 mm, port margin 2.0 mm | 24.0 × 18.8 × 1.754 mm, trace centre y = 1.85 mm, stub centre x = 12.0 mm |

The openEMS record states its own comparability
(`meta.comparability`): its S11/S21 PHASES are referenced at the tutorial's
measurement planes, `MeasPlaneShift = MSL_length/3 = 16.667 mm` in from each
port face on 50 mm arms, and are not comparable with a record taken on another
arm length; the MAGNITUDES are. The case compares magnitudes only and measures
that premise on rfx itself (the arm-length witness in the ladder test).

Neither file is re-generated by CI. The external solvers are run by hand, when
the case is created or its geometry changes.

`stage_b_coarse` carries `stage_a`'s arrays rather than a second solve
(`meta.stages.stage_b_coarse.reused_from = "stage_a"`): the geometry delta is
empty, so that rung IS the tutorial's own mesh and solving it twice would put
two identical arrays in the record.

The JUDGED reference is `stage_b_fine`. `stage_a` (= `stage_b_coarse`) and
`stage_b_mid` are the record's own mesh statement.

### Its passivity witness

`meta.passivity_witness`: `max(|S11|² + |S21|²)` over the **witness band only**,
2–7 GHz, with `passivity_tol = 1.05`. The CalcPort grid starts at 1 MHz, where
both port voltages sit at the numerical floor and their ratio is not an
S-parameter the structure produced. Every bin's energy sum is still recorded,
with its maximum over the band, over the whole grid, and over 0–1 GHz and
1–2 GHz separately.
