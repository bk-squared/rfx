# Where the frozen references for the Sheen low-pass filter come from

Facts only.

## Which file describes which board

The board is the same everywhere: lossless RT/Duroid εr = 2.2, h_sub = 0.794 mm;
two 2.413 mm wide 50 Ω feeds, their centres 6.607 mm apart across the board
(y = 9.8565 mm and y = 16.4635 mm), joined by one 20.320 × 2.540 mm wide
low-impedance section at x = 12.466 – 15.006 mm. Coordinates from
Elsherbeni-Demir Sec. 6.2 as transcribed in roseengineering/rffdtd's
`examples/lowpass.py`, which reproduces D. M. Sheen, S. M. Ali, M. D. Abouzahra
and J. A. Kong, IEEE Trans. MTT 38(7):849-857, July 1990.

| record | box | ports |
|---|---|---|
| `openems_sheen.json` | 27.472 × 26.320 × 3.794 mm | two openEMS MSLPorts on the x faces, `FeedShift` 2.5 mm on port 1, `MeasPlaneShift` 5.610 mm on both |
| `palace_fem.json` | the same frame | two 50 Ω LUMPED ports (ground → strip, +z) at x = 2.5 and 24.972 mm |
| the case's rfx runs (`test_sheen_lpf.py`) | the same box, the openEMS record's own | two rfx MSL ports at x = 2.5 and 24.972 mm, probes 6 mm upstream |

The openEMS record states its own comparability (`meta.comparability`): its
S11/S21 PHASES are referenced at this board's own measurement planes,
0.45 × 12.466 mm = 5.610 mm in from each port face, and are not comparable with
a record taken on a different box or a different `MeasPlaneShift`; the
MAGNITUDES are. The case compares magnitudes only. rfx solves the same box, so
there is no arm-length premise to measure here.

Neither file is re-generated by CI (`meta.ci_runs_this = false`). The external
solvers are run by hand, when the case is created or its geometry changes.

### The tutorial reproduction (Stage A gate)

Stage A is openEMS's own `MSL_NotchFilter` tutorial, run verbatim in every job
before any rung of this board is solved. It is a DIFFERENT structure on a
different substrate — the record says so itself (`meta.stage_a_is`) — and it is
never compared with the Sheen board.

| quantity | value |
|---|---|
| measured notch | 3.672242 GHz |
| measured depth | −53.068 dB |
| analytic notch | 3.687193 GHz |
| deviation | 0.405 % |
| gate band | 2.949755 – 3.871553 GHz |
| minimum depth | −20 dB |
| passed | yes (`f_notch_ok`, `depth_ok`) |

A separate earlier reproduction of the same tutorial is recorded in
`meta.reproduce_gate_record`: 3.671101 GHz against the analytic 3.687193 GHz,
0.436 %, VESSL run 369367251705, log
`validation/crossval/_20_msl_phase_referee_logs/20260804T070702Z_run.log`
lines 10-17, verified 2026-08-04.

**Stage A ran three times, once per part, and the merge accepts the spread**
(`meta.stage_a_reproducibility`). openEMS on 8 threads does not cross the 1e-5
energy criterion at the same timestep twice, so the three jobs ended Stage A at
14 586 / 14 688 / 12 342 steps. Measured spread against the first part, over
2-7 GHz: |Δ|S11|| ≤ 1.31e-4, |Δ|S21|| ≤ 1.56e-4 (tolerance 1e-3 linear), notch
spread 0.00097 % (tolerance 0.01 %). Phase is not compared — at the notch the
runs sit either side of a 180° wrap. The merged record carries the FIRST part's
Stage A arrays.

### The three rungs

The rung factor scales the x lines, the y lines, the air above the board AND
the substrate's own z lines, so the 794 µm board carries more cells per rung in
the direction that sets the effective permittivity. The fine rung DECLARED 8
substrate cells and REALIZED 10: CSXCAD's own `SmoothMeshLines` added two
lines. The realized number is what the table below and the case's fast test
carry.

Null shifts: coarse → mid **+0.332 %**, mid → fine **+0.218 %**.

The JUDGED reference is `stage_b_fine`. `stage_b_coarse` and `stage_b_mid` are
the record's own mesh statement.

**The null names one member of a pair.** This board's stopband is a DOUBLE
transmission zero near 7 and 8 GHz. The estimator
(`validation/crossval/comparators/spectral_features.py::refined_extremum`,
`transform='log'`, band 5-10 GHz) reports the DEEPEST minimum in that band, and
which member that is can change between meshes and solvers. The whole 801-bin
curve is in the record; the case prints it.

### Why the record is truncated, and what stands in for a decay level

Every Stage B rung stopped at a DECLARED record length (`--real-nrts`
60 000 / 84 853 / 120 000, about 10 ns) rather than at an energy criterion. The
tutorial's default 1e-5 did not finish: two uncapped runs (369367263243,
369367263269) passed 107 minutes on the coarsest rung alone. The reason is in
the record — the box energy sits FLAT at about −31 dB from N to 2N steps
(`final_energy_db` −31.27 / −30.49 / −31.83), a trapped mode that no end
criterion closes.

What says the declared length is long enough is
`meta.record_length_witness` / `stage_b_<rung>.witness_2n`: every rung was
solved TWICE, at N and 2N timesteps, and the longer run's arrays are in the
record beside the difference. Over 2-12 GHz, counting only bins where both
curves are above −20 dB:

| rung | N → 2N steps | \|ΔS21\| | at | \|ΔS11\| | at | bins compared |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `coarse` | 60 000 → 120 000 | 5.50e-4 dB | 5.4238 GHz | 1.38e-3 dB | 5.4238 GHz | 317 / 402 |
| `mid` | 84 853 → 169 706 | 1.51e-5 dB | 6.3988 GHz | 9.46e-5 dB | 7.3494 GHz | 317 / 402 |
| `fine` | 120 000 → 240 000 | 1.09e-3 dB | 8.7631 GHz | 8.18e-4 dB | 8.8119 GHz | 317 / 402 |

The case prints these six numbers and holds them against 0.1 dB — one
twentieth of the 2 dB magnitude bar, a sanity bound on the premise, not a gate
on the physics. The record itself states them as reported, not gated.

### Its passivity witness, and the deficit it does not judge

`meta.passivity_witness`: `max(|S11|² + |S21|²)` over the **witness band only**,
2-12 GHz, with `passivity_tol = 1.05`. The witness bounds the EXCESS above
unity; it says nothing about a sum BELOW unity. On this board the sum runs
0.78 - 1.00 across 2-12 GHz and falls to 0.41 by 20 GHz, and that deficit is
RECORDED per bin (`energy_sum`, plus the band and whole-grid minima and the
maxima above the band) and not judged in the record.

| stage | 2-12 GHz | whole 0.5-20 GHz grid |
|---|---|---|
| `stage_b_coarse` | 0.782937 – 1.000151 | min 0.413463 |
| `stage_b_mid` | 0.785724 – 1.000202 | min 0.440703 |
| `stage_b_fine` | 0.786526 – 1.000174 | min 0.459049 |

That band edge, 12 GHz, is where the case reads both curves.

## `validation/crossval/_07_sheen_results/openems.json` — removed 2026-09-23

The previous openEMS record was removed with the case that produced it. It
carried eight fields — solver, `res_um` 198.5, `runtime_s` 172.1 and the five
801-point arrays — and named no openEMS version, no build, no run id and no
boundary; its `reproduce_gate_record` was absent, while the producing script's
own gate record said `status: "UNRUN"`.

Its numbers, measured from its arrays with the estimators above before it was
deleted: stopband null **7.994749 GHz** at **−43.749 dB**, passband mean
**−0.814 dB**, −3 dB corner **5.541030 GHz**, Re(Z0) median **51.352 Ω**,
energy sum **0.783015 – 1.001861** over 2-12 GHz and **0.413432** at 20 GHz.

The new `stage_b_coarse` rung is the same resolution (198.5 µm) with a
60 000-step declared record instead of a 30 000-step cap, and it reproduces
that record: over 2-12 GHz where both curves are above −20 dB, |Δ|S21|| ≤
**0.0095 dB** (317 bins) and |Δ|S11|| ≤ **0.0072 dB** (402 bins); over the whole
801-bin grid, 0.068 dB and 0.030 dB.

## The stopband features

Every estimate below is the vertex of a parabola fitted through the minimum bin
and its two neighbours in log|S| (the same vertex as a fit in dB) —
`validation/crossval/comparators/spectral_features.py::refined_extremum`.

| record | deepest null in 5-10 GHz (GHz) | depth (dB) | the pair, where the record names both |
|---|---|---|---|
| openEMS `stage_b_coarse`, 198.5 µm | 7.993871 | −43.806 | — |
| openEMS `stage_b_mid`, 140.4 µm | 8.020443 | −43.739 | — |
| openEMS `stage_b_fine`, 99.25 µm (JUDGED) | 8.037921 | −43.730 | — |
| Palace `coarse`, lc 0.25 mm | 7.019017 | −50.907 | 7.019017 and 8.026106 |
| Palace `mid`, lc 0.18 mm | 8.048223 | −51.159 | 7.031752 and 8.048223 |
| (removed) openEMS 198.5 µm, 30 000-step cap | 7.994749 | −43.749 | — |

The two solvers' deepest-minimum readings are not the same member of the pair
on every mesh: Palace `coarse` names the ~7 GHz zero and Palace `mid` the
~8 GHz one, while every openEMS rung names the ~8 GHz one. That is a fact about
the estimator's window, not a disagreement between the solvers about where the
zeros are; the curves are in both records.
