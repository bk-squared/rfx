# Where the frozen reference for the RT/Duroid 5880 patch antenna comes from

Facts only.

## Which file describes which board

The board is the same everywhere: a 39.7 × 49.25 mm rectangular patch
(39.7 mm along x, the resonant length) on 3.175 mm of RT/Duroid 5880 (εr 2.2,
tanδ 0.001, taken as a conductivity at 2.4 GHz, 2.9374e-4 S/m) over a
55.6 × 65.1 mm ground; the substrate covers the ground's footprint; both metals
have zero thickness; a 50 Ω lumped probe runs from the ground to the patch
8.73125 mm off the patch centre along x. These are the constants of the retired
script `validation/crossval/15_patch_antenna_rt5880.py` (removed 2026-09-24),
frozen in the maker's `RETIRED_CONSTANT_LINES`, except five, each in the
maker's `DECLARED_CONSTANT_DEPARTURES`:

- the probe (delta 9): the retired script put it 9 mm off centre, and the PI
  moved it on 2026-09-24 to −8.73125 mm, a lattice node on every rung of the
  rfx ladder (−11, −22 and −33 cells of h/4, h/8 and h/12);
- the patch and the ground (delta 10): the retired 40 × 50 mm patch over a
  56 × 66 mm ground became 39.7 × 49.25 mm over 55.6 × 65.1 mm (PI
  2026-09-25), every edge just outside a node common to h/4, h/8 and h/12
  (half-extents 25, 31, 35 and 41 cells of h/4 plus 6.25, 18.75, 18.75 and
  6.25 µm; the patch centre is a node). openEMS's air box did not follow the
  ground: its faces stay at the retired box's x ±88, y ±93 mm.

| record | box | absorber | probe |
|---|---|---|---|
| `openems_patch.json` | ground centred at the origin; absorber inner faces at x ±88, y ±93, z −40 / +90 mm (60.2 mm clear of the ground's x edges and 60.45 mm of its y edges, 40 mm below it, 86.8 mm above the patch) | `PML_8` on all six faces, laid outside those faces, 21.8 – 22.0 / 15.5 – 15.6 / 10.8 – 11.1 mm deep on the three rungs | `AddLumpedPort`, 50 Ω, z = 0 → 3.175 mm at x = −8.73125 mm, y = 0 |
| the case's rfx runs (`test_rt5880_patch.py`) | the patch centred at (38.100, 44.450) mm in a 76.200 × 88.900 × 28.575 mm declared domain: 10.3 mm clear of the ground's x edges, 11.9 mm of its y edges, 9.525 mm below it, 15.875 mm above the patch | CPML 19.05 mm deep outside the domain on every face: 24 / 48 / 72 cells at h/4 / h/8 / h/12 | `add_port(component="ez", extent=3.175 mm)`, a 50 Ω wire port from the ground sheet's plane to the patch sheet's |

The two boxes are not the same. How far rfx's box and absorber move the
resonance was measured on the retired 40 × 50 mm board at h/4 and tabulated in
the case's own file at commit `0a92af49` (the comment above `AIR_H`); on every
ladder run the case also solves h/4 once in
this record's own box (the declared domain's faces on the absorber inner faces
above, each snapped to the nearest h/4 node: x ±88.106, y ±92.869,
z −39.688 / +89.694 mm; the ladder's 19.05 mm CPML outside) and reports how far
the resonance and |S11| move against the ladder box, without judging it.

The record is not re-generated by CI (`meta.ci_runs_this = false`). The
external solver is run by hand, when the case is created or its geometry
changes.

and the substrate's own z cells; the air box does not scale.

`refined_remax` in `tests/crossval/_v2_judging.py`: the largest Re(Zin) bin in
the record's window, the parabola vertex through it and its neighbours, R the
parabola's value, X Im(Zin) interpolated at the vertex). Read from the same
arrays, and pinned by the case's fast test on `stage_b_fine`:

| stage | f0 (Re(Zin) maximum) | R(f0) | X(f0) |
|---|---|---|---|
| `stage_b_coarse` | 2.313059 GHz | 72.294 Ω | +36.420 Ω |
| `stage_b_mid` | 2.318115 GHz | 72.857 Ω | +39.174 Ω |
| `stage_b_fine` | 2.319490 GHz | 73.457 Ω | +42.953 Ω |

f0 shifts: coarse → mid **+0.219 %**, mid → fine **+0.059 %**.

The JUDGED reference is `stage_b_fine`. `stage_b_coarse` and `stage_b_mid` are
the record's own mesh statement.

**The rungs are not a uniform √2 refinement near the patch edges.** The
thirds-rule pair cell scales 0.600 → 0.424 → 0.300 mm, but the cells either
side of it do not scale alike (`meta.stages.<rung>.edge_table_realized` lists
the lines at each edge). The record's rung-to-rung steps are therefore not
extrapolated.

### Its passivity witness

`meta.passivity_witness`: `max |S11|²` over each stage's whole grid, with
`passivity_tol = 1.05`. A radiating patch's |S11|² sits below one by the power
it accepts; the per-bin values (`s11_power`) and the band minimum are recorded.

## What the record does not contain

- No comparison with rfx and no verdict on the board (`meta.what_is_not_here`).
- No record-length witness of the N/2N kind: every Stage B rung ended on its
  1e-5 energy criterion instead (end energy −51.8 / −50.1 / −50.1 dB).
- No far-field pattern arrays: only the directivity at the resonance
  (`farfield.dmax_dbi`); the NF2FF dumps were measured and deleted in the pod.
- No mesh finer than `stage_b_fine`, and no statement of where the thirds rule
  puts the patch's electrical edge on each rung.
