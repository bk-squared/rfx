## `docs/agent/recipe-rt-measurement.mdx` — section (d) is wrong and must go

Current text (lines ~140-145):

> ### (d) Cell-alignment nudge for slab thickness
>
> `Box` uses inclusive bounds (`coords >= lo and coords <= hi`), so a
> physically `d`-thick slab can map to `d + dx` cells. Nudge the upper
> x-corner down by `dx/2` to hit the last interior cell:
> `slab_x_hi = center + d/2 - dx/2`.

`Box` does NOT use inclusive bounds. `rfx/geometry/csg.py` documents the volume
branch as half-open `[lo, hi)` over node coordinates, and #931 leaves
dielectric sampling exactly there. Replace with:

> ### (d) Draw the slab at its corners, on the lattice
>
> A dielectric `Box` is sampled at NODE coordinates, half-open `[lo, hi)`: a
> slab drawn `lo = i*dx` to `hi = k*dx` owns nodes `i .. k-1` and realizes
> exactly `hi - lo`. No nudge is needed or wanted.
>
> What DOES bite is the float route to the corner. `center + d/2` can land one
> ulp above the node that `hi` is meant to be, the half-open test then keeps
> that node, and the slab realizes one cell too many against an oracle
> evaluated at `d`. Spell on-lattice corners in lattice arithmetic —
> `i_lo = round((center - d/2)/dx)`, `x_lo = i_lo*dx`,
> `x_hi = (i_lo + round(d/dx))*dx` — and assert the realized extent from
> `sim.fidelity_report()` before quoting anything against a Fresnel oracle.
> `examples/tutorials/slab_rt_flux_monitor.py` does both.

## `docs/public/guide/materials-geometry.mdx`

The Docs brief already owns the sheet-vs-volume section. The two worked
contrasts written for `examples/tutorials/boundary_spec_demo.py` are reusable
verbatim:

* a ground plane declared `add_thin_conductor(Box((0,0,z0), (Lx,Ly,z0)))`
  realizes ONE wall plane at `z0` and adds no cell to the stack-up;
* a 2 mm plate declared `add(Box((0,0,z0), (Lx,Ly,z0+2mm)), material="pec")`
  realizes walls at `z0` AND `z0+2mm` with the two cells between them shorted.
