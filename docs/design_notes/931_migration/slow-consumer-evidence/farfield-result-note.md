## Causal controls

The complete actual historical route was verified in git source: uniform and nonuniform API material assembly wrote a node-sampled `pec_mask`, leaving vacuum material coefficients for PEC; both field lanes applied the historical `C & (backward-neighbor | forward-neighbor)` component rule. The diagnostic restores exactly that edge rule, including its boundary convention. It is not the conductivity fold used in the separate waveguide witness.

The node-sampled historical occupancy and current center-sampled occupancy have **XOR zero** on both meshes, so classification/sampling contributes no changed cells in this fixture. Current versus historical edge counts are 294 versus 216 per component on the uniform mesh; 1092/1092/1014 versus 864/864/864 on the graded mesh. The historical high x/y wall is 11.5 mm on uniform but 11.625 mm on graded; #931 realizes the declared 11.75 mm on both. Thus the fixture comment claiming relative power gates are unaffected by the added far faces is contradicted by a controlled measurement.

All within-mesh operator A/B controls match: dt, shape, occupied cells, source indices and physical coordinates, source dual volume, source waveform samples/spectrum, acquisition length, and NTFF indices/coordinates. Across meshes:

* Source location is (11, 11, 9.5) mm to floating-point roundoff; NTFF boundaries are 4.5 and 17.5 mm on every axis.
* dt is 0.476643717383 ps uniform versus 0.275190378538 ps graded. Six hundred steps span 285.986230430 versus 165.114227123 ps. These differences were not changed by the diagnostic.
* Current-source dual volumes are 1.5625e-11 versus 3.90625055661e-12 m³, with the existing production current normalization using the local volume. These are identical across operator A/B.
* The dt-weighted 30 GHz source spectra differ by only 2.59786961e-5 relative complex amplitude across meshes; source spectral power ratio is 1.00002670755. The final waveform is zero in uniform and −1.2787e-9 in graded. This rejects a grossly truncated injected pulse as an explanation for the 6% power difference; it does not prove field ring-down convergence.
* Independent host NumPy versus JAX NTFF integration has maximum angular intensity error, relative to peak, between 6.84e-7 and 1.12e-6 in all four cases. This rules out a materially different JAX-only integration result, while both extractors still share the same NTFF records.

## Full trace inspection and remaining limits

All 300 angular bins of Etheta/Ephi and intensity, and all six complex NTFF face arrays, are finite in every case. All four P metrics are positive and nonzero. The saved and visually inspected `farfield-attribution.png` shows every angular intensity bin for both meshes under both operators, plus their peak-normalized difference maps. It shows continuous broad polar lobes and pronounced mesh-dependent azimuthal modulation under both operators; the discrepancy is not one bad bin or an all-zero record.

**The earlier 2.1% integrated-power agreement did not imply angular-pattern equivalence.** The maximum difference between peak-normalized patterns is 0.29772270 current and 0.29167879 historical. Relative L2 difference after normalization by total P is 0.18730822 current and 0.18837702 historical. Thus roughly 19% angular shape disagreement already coexists with the historical passing power metric. The ownership intervention isolates why this scalar envelope was crossed, not all pre-existing cross-mesh angular discrepancies.

Both meshes keep their original finite acquisition windows, which have different physical durations. A negligible source tail does not establish a converged scattered field or NTFF integral; this diagnostic has no temporal convergence study or independent physical referee. The graded profile also retains its original abrupt adjacent-cell ratio 2.5 and the existing in-plane-grading advisory. Neither duration nor grading should be adjusted ad hoc to restore the gate. Qualification of a redesigned physical envelope or a different purpose-specific integration test is a PI decision. The current #931 operator, 5% gate and fixture remain unchanged.
