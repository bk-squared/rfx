## Implementation

`forward(gradient="adjoint")` selects a custom VJP around the uniform scan.
The forward stores E and curl H on the resolved design write window; the
reverse scan transposes the production carry step and its observation outputs.
The existing coefficient pullback supplies eps and optional sigma gradients.
No whole-domain carry history or checkpoint replay is used by this path.
The default remains `gradient="autodiff"`.

Derivation and per-path table: [design note](20261001-1424-discrete-adjoint.md).
Debye/Lorentz, graded, ports, ring-down and distributed are refused in this commit.
