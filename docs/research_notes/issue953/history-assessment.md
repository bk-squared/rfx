Thus this particular reported jump is a change in the recorded spectral feature, not a changed reference denominator or a 31-fold physical frequency shift. Import4bee84f3 is not an established original producer SHA; post259191's `commit.txt` is empty. [Retained provenance limits](../../../docs/research_notes/issue953/README.md#L62).

- “Old Box” must not be interpreted as a correctly realized finite-thickness modern PEC volume. The d990 path declares trace/stub as `H_SUB→H_SUB+DX`, with domain-face PEC ground, but its operator is `C & (backward | forward)`. A one-layer normal component remains live; an in-plane last occupied index can retain its outgoing edge. Modern #931 sheets require both edge endpoints in the closed footprint, and modern volumes have a different incidence rule. A current volume-versus-sheet A/B cannot reproduce that old operator. [d990 declaration](https://github.com/bk-squared/rfx/blob/d990e18ce0870ac7a893a86627e7232d1cf92c7b/validation/crossval/06b_msl_notch_filter_uniform.py#L411-L439), [d990 mask](https://github.com/bk-squared/rfx/blob/d990e18ce0870ac7a893a86627e7232d1cf92c7b/rfx/boundaries/pec.py#L169-L173), [#931 sheet rule](../../../docs/design_notes/20260906_plan_realign_lattice_ownership.md#L76). Do not retroactively assign the modern four-interval sheet interpretation as the complete pre-#931 electromagnetic geometry.

- The migration's own simple “one-cell shortening explains the shift” prediction was falsified even on baseline (+0.51% predicted versus +3.67% observed). The claim that electrical-width choice explains “most” of the error is an interpretation, not an isolated result. [Falsified predictions](../../../validation/crossval/_06b_msl_notch_results/RECOMPUTE.md#L100), [uncontrolled width interpretation](../../../validation/crossval/_06b_msl_notch_results/RECOMPUTE.md#L113). Old snapshots also precede later source-span/current-observation fixes. The actual old per-drive source, observation and PEC signatures are not available as a matched control pair.
