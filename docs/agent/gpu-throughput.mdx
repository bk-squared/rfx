# GPU throughput — measured numbers and what they mean

Every figure here is a marginal-cost measurement from
`scripts/diagnostics/gpu_throughput_bench.py`: two runs of different step
counts are timed and differenced, so compilation, host dispatch and
buffer setup subtract out and what remains is the compiled scan's
per-step cost. Timing a whole `run()` instead reads several times too
LOW at small grids (fixed cost dominates) — a ladder that RISES with grid
size is the signature of that mistake.

## The boundary condition is part of the number

A CPML absorber updates auxiliary fields in its pad shells and enlarges
the padded grid, so an open-boundary step costs more than a closed box.
Measured 2026-10-26 on an RTX 4090 (float32, a cube with one soft source,
this harness's method), ms per step:

| interior cells | boundary | uniform | graded (4:1 dz) | padded cells |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 13.1 M | PEC walls | 1.578 | 1.529 | 13.1 M |
| 13.1 M | CPML, 4 layers | 2.128 | 2.133 | 14.5 M |
| 13.1 M | CPML, 8 layers | 2.395 | 2.427 | 16.0 M |
| 13.1 M | CPML, 16 layers | 3.124 | 3.171 | 19.2 M |
| 27.3 M | PEC walls | 3.195 | 3.186 | 27.3 M |
| 27.3 M | CPML, 4 layers | 3.905 | 3.944 | 29.5 M |
| 27.3 M | CPML, 8 layers | 4.467 | 4.495 | 31.9 M |
| 27.3 M | CPML, 16 layers | 5.667 | 5.677 | 36.9 M |

Step time with CPML divided by step time of the PEC box of the same
interior (t_CPML / t_PEC from the table): 4 layers 1.22-1.40x and
8 layers 1.40-1.59x over 13.1 M and 27.3 M cells, uniform and graded.
Most of that is the extra padded cells; an
absorbing cell costs about one more bare cell, plus a few percent per
padded cell. The PEC rows are the standard GPU lane: a closed box today
takes the GPU fast lane, which measured 1.988 ms (13.1 M) and 3.871 ms
(27.3 M) on this card, slower than the standard lane.

Quote the PEC figure only for closed-cavity work. Antenna, scattering
and any open-boundary problem pays the absorber, so the CPML rows are the
honest number for those.

(Before the absorber update was confined to each face's layers and its
GPU write-back fused into the field update, 2026-10-08/09, the same card
measured 7,266 Mcells/s with PEC walls against 2,087 with 8 CPML layers on
a 200³ cube, 3.5x. That figure no longer describes the code.)

## Cards, CPML lane

Measured 2026-08-25 on the same harness, before the absorber changes of

| Mcells/s | 300³ bare | 400³ bare | 512³ bare | 300³ nu-z | 400³ nu-z | 512³ nu-z |

## Kernel rate is not production rate

A production S-parameter run carries per-step DFT accumulation at every
port bin, probe bookkeeping and lumped elements. Measured on a 256 M-cell
board run: **628 Mcells/s on the H200**, against 4,350 for the bare
kernel at a comparable size — a 7x gap that is path overhead, not the
kernel. The gap grows with card speed (the A6000's was 1.3x), so a faster
card buys less than its kernel ratio suggests until that path is
addressed.
