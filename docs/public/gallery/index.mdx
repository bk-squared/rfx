## A closed-form check

The [empty WR-90 waveguide](/rfx/gallery/waveguide_wr90/) compares a matched
rectangular guide in the X band with its closed form: across the band `|S21|`
sits at about 0 dB and `|S11|` stays below 0.0062. It was computed with rfx 2.0.0rc1 at `9f1abcc`; the page
gives the measurement definitions and limits.

## Teaching examples

Three single-mesh field movies, a pulse meeting open and reflecting boundaries,
a dielectric interface and a closed cavity, are now

convergence or accuracy benchmarks.
