## Baseline geometry

Place the ground plane as a finite conductor inside the domain, not as a full-domain PEC boundary wall. For foil that is a sheet — `add_thin_conductor()` or a zero-thickness `Box` — and a `Box` with real thickness only when the metal really is that thick ([How conductors land on the lattice](/rfx/guide/materials-geometry/#how-conductors-land-on-the-lattice)). A finite ground plane radiates into the absorber beneath it, which is the open-radiation problem being modelled; a PEC bottom wall turns the structure into a closed cavity and shifts the resonance high.

## Interpretation rules

- Patch accuracy sits in the `gate_paths` of `validation/crossval/manifest.json` — `tests/locks/test_patch_edgefed_resonance_harminv.py`, `tests/locks/test_patch_edgefed_s11_passivity.py`, `tests/oracle/test_patch_cavity_eps_oracle.py`, `tests/crossval/test_patch_canonical_farfield_e4.py`.
- Use impedance-referenced S-parameter results only through a documented port workflow — see [Sources & Ports](/rfx/guide/sources-ports/) for `add_port`.
- Treat non-uniform mesh variants as limited support unless the page you are following says otherwise.
