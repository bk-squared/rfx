This page summarizes user-visible changes.

## Current public status

The recommended starting point remains the **uniform Cartesian Yee RF/FDTD lane**. Non-default lanes such as non-uniform mesh are documented through their [support and evidence envelopes](/rfx/guide/validation/), not presented as the default path.

## Unreleased — 2.0.0

**Breaking: how a conductor lands on the lattice.** A conductor is now exactly
one of three things and your declaration says which — a **volume** of primal
cells (`sim.add(shape, material="pec")`), a **sheet** on one node plane
(`sim.add_thin_conductor(...)`, or a zero-thickness `Box` through `sim.add()`),
or a **wire** (a sub-cell `PolylineWire`). One sentence covers all three: an E
component is PEC if and only if its own location lies inside the closed
conductor region. See
[How conductors land on the lattice](/rfx/guide/materials-geometry/#how-conductors-land-on-the-lattice).

- **Removed: the `two_plane=True` keyword on `sim.add()`.** Passing it raises
  `TypeError`; there is no deprecation period and no replacement keyword. It
  existed to give a one-cell PEC body its far face, and the volume rule now does
  that at every thickness, on every axis, on every lane.
- **A PEC volume realizes walls at both of its drawn faces** and shorts every
  normal edge between them, so realized thickness equals drawn thickness. PEC
  volumes are sampled at cell centres rather than at nodes, which rounds each
  face to the nearest plane instead of always dropping the `hi` one. Recipes that
  compensated for the missing far face — a drawn extent one cell longer than the
  physical one, a conductor drawn one cell outside the interface it was meant to
  bound, "interior faces on cell midpoints" as a *requirement* — are no longer
  needed and have been deleted rather than re-tuned. **Dielectric sampling is
  unchanged** (node coordinates, half-open), so a model with no conductor body is
  bit-identical to 1.x.
- **A sheet owns no cell.** It adds nothing to the PEC cell mask, writes no εᵣ or
  σ, and is realized on exactly one node plane — the plane nearest the shape's
  mid-plane, with an exact half-cell tie resolving to the lower plane. The E
  component normal to a sheet stays live. Sheets on one plane are unioned before
  the edge rule, so abutting sheets realize seamlessly. A lossy
  (`surface_impedance_f0`) sheet uses the same footprint and the same edge set as
  the PEC sheet, so `f0` toggles loss and never geometry.
- **New refusals, raised when the grid exists** — at `run()`, `forward()` or
  `preflight()`, not at `add()`, because "thinner than one cell" is a question
  about the lattice and `add()` has no grid to ask it of. A PEC shape with
  `0 < extent < one local cell` along any axis raises — declare a sheet or resolve the thickness. A PEC
  shape that rasterizes to zero cells raises instead of vanishing silently. A
  `Box` with two or three zero-extent axes raises. `add_thin_conductor()` with a
  shape thicker than one local cell along its normal raises.
- The plane-selection tie rule shipped in 1.x ("a window selecting exactly one
  node — that node is the realized plane") **survives** as the sheet
  plane-selection rule, so an existing one-cell sheet declaration lands where it
  landed before.
- `fidelity_report()` prints, per PEC entry, the drawn extent against the
  realized wall planes on each axis, in your input units.
- The design-interop IR is at v2: the required `two_plane` field is gone and
  sheets are expressed directly. A v1 document carrying `two_plane` is refused
  with a message rather than silently ignored.

Superseded by the above: the #493 waveguide-obstacle drawing recipe (realized
extent one cell short of drawn, at the `hi` face; an electrical opening of
`d + dx` or `d + 2·dx`), the #740 `two_plane` ground fix for the RT5880 patch,
the #674 "exactly one cell layer" sheet requirement, and the #702 re-sampling of
a sheet's "own cell" material — a sheet has no own cell to re-sample. Their
CHANGELOG entries are left as dated history.

- A face-touching dispersive material no longer gets an absorber surround
  carrying its static permittivity without its dispersion poles — a material
  no declared model has. The `dispersive_pole_at_absorber_face` preflight
  advisory now fires for every pole family touching an absorbing face and
  states what the pad actually contains.
- Realized geometry at node-aligned faces no longer depends on
  `JAX_ENABLE_X64` or the solver lane: one exact float64 node formula is
  shared by the uniform lane, the non-uniform lane, and the viewer, and a
  one-cell sheet realizes on one and the same plane in both lanes. (At 2.0 the
  node/half-open statement describes dielectric sampling only; PEC volumes are
  centre-sampled. The one-node tie rule became the sheet plane-selection rule.)
- New preflight warning `pec_face_short_of_domain_wall`. A domain is realized
  by `ceil(extent / dx)` cells while a PEC volume's face rounds to the nearest
  node, so a conductor drawn to a declared dimension can land one cell short of
  the wall it was meant to reach — and that cell is a parallel-plate line along
  the wall, open at both ends, not a short. The check reads the entry's own
  realized wall planes against the grid's interior, on non-absorbing faces only.
  Draw a conductor meant to reach a wall to the grid's REALIZED wall plane.
- Fixed: on the distributed non-uniform lane, a conductor cell sitting on a rank
  boundary reached its neighbour un-zeroed. The E ghost rows were exchanged
  before the PEC stages, which act on a rank's own cells only, so the neighbour
  read a stale copy of the conductor's plane; the exchange now runs last in the
  E half-step. Single-device results are unaffected.
- A `dz_profile`-only simulation now dispatches to the non-uniform waveguide
  S-matrix lane instead of silently solving on the uniform grid built from
  the scalar `dx`. The unsupported default `normalize=False` fails loudly
  there, naming `dz_profile`. dz-graded accuracy remains unvalidated; the
  non-uniform lane reports the same ring-down settling witness as the
  uniform lane.

## v1.7.0 — September 1, 2026

- Long solves can report step count, elapsed time, rate, and ETA with
  `report_every=N` on supported uniform and single-device non-uniform runs.
  Reporting is off by default and the reported and unreported results are
  byte-identity locked.
- `Simulation.fidelity_report()` audits declared geometry against the cells and
  material realization the solver will use, before a run. It reports
  rasterization and realization only; it does not predict RF error.
- `add_msl_port(direction=...)` supports `"+x"`, `"-x"`, `"+y"`, and `"-y"`
  for the Laplace/quasi-TEM path. The eigenmode path remains x-only and outside
  the public support envelope.
- `add_thin_conductor(..., surface_impedance_f0=...)` accepts patterned shapes
  that rasterize to exactly one cell layer and at least one cell, including
  CAD-backed `MeshShape` objects. (Superseded at 2.0: a sheet is one node plane,
  not one cell layer.) Unsupported sheet realizations and execution
  lanes fail explicitly.
- The uniform single-device solver offers `precision="float64"` when JAX x64
  is enabled. `precision="mixed"` works with CPML, but its float16 field
  storage raises the absorber residual floor and is not a substitute for
  float32 in low-reflection or S-parameter work.
- `compute_far_field_jax(..., max_phase_bytes=...)` bounds the NTFF phase-array
  allocation by splitting the theta grid. The split is exact and avoids
  materializing the full direction-by-surface array at once.
- S-parameter settling warnings use the same -40 dB rule in
  `compute_msl_s_matrix`, `compute_waveguide_s_matrix`,
  `compute_mixed_s_matrix`, `compute_coaxial_two_port`, and
  `compute_coax_msl_transition`.
- Multi-band graded-z meshes now preserve declared blocks through smoothing,
  with a measured z-axis adjacent-cell ratio cap of 1.4 and explicit
  preflight advisories outside the validated envelope.
- New rasterized-slice and stack-profile views show the cells and material
  layers the solver actually builds.
- Wire-port decomposition, MSL probe extraction, CPML material padding,
  graded-mesh NTFF integration, mesh import containment, and preflight
  diagnostics received correctness fixes without widening their documented
  validation envelopes.
- MSL internal DFT planes now store only the extractor's required crop,
  reducing accumulator cells without changing the extracted matrix.
- Differentiable `forward(...)` objectives are now safe to wrap in an outer `jax.jit`: both `jax.jit(loss)` and `jax.jit(jax.grad(loss))` over `forward(eps_override=...)` are regression-locked against the eager path. This fixes a tracer crash in material assembly without changing eager results.
- `optimize(...)` gained default-off robustness knobs for difficult proxy losses: `n_starts`, `best_iterate`, `step_clamp`, and `seed`. The default single-start Adam path remains byte-identical.
- `add_lumped_rlc(...)` elements now participate in the differentiable uniform `forward(...)` lane. Use `forward(rlc_values_override={...})` when R/L/C values themselves are scalar design variables; this is still a lumped circuit element, not a `Z0`-referenced port.
- Waveguide S-matrix extraction now emits a soft advisory for passive `normalize=False` results whose column power is above the documented single-run over-unity envelope but below the hard unreliability limit. The physics and hard pass/fail thresholds are unchanged.
- RCS and inverse-design docs now pin recent footguns: RCS validation is monostatic-only unless additional evidence is supplied, and a finite-difference gradient check does not prove an empty observation window is physically meaningful.
- A standalone real multi-device witness (`scripts/diagnostics/distributed_witness/multigpu_witness.py`) now compares single-device and sharded PEC/CPML/lumped-port runs on machines with two or more JAX devices; it is a distributed-runner identity check, not a new physics-validation envelope.
- [AD memory-planning docs](/rfx/guide/memory-reduction/) now tag each artifact with an evidence class — static estimate, conservative budget-fit plan, or explainability breakdown — so a planning estimate is never read as a runtime profiler measurement, a peak-memory guarantee, or RF validation evidence. The compiled-executable check reports the JAX compiler's memory *estimate*, not a runtime peak.
- [Differentiable-design guidance](/rfx/guide/autodiff-adjoint/) was tightened: it separates JAX-traced objectives and finite-difference gradient checks from the final port/resonance/far-field physics validation.
- [Non-uniform mesh guidance](/rfx/guide/nonuniform-mesh/) now frames graded-z meshes as a bounded workflow for thin layered structures, stating the unsupported combinations and the caller's validation responsibilities up front.

## v1.6.7 — July 2026
- Open-domain oblique RCS is available through `compute_rcs(theta_inc != 0)` for its documented uniform-grid, `ez`-polarized Method-B TFSF envelope; unsupported combinations fail explicitly.
- The opt-in plane-wave design lane adds complex-Bloch TFSF in `run()`, uniform `forward()` support, differentiable `mu_r_override`, and `compute_rcs_jax`.
- `rfx.MeshShape` imports voxelized STL, OBJ, and PLY geometry; STEP support is available with the `cad` extra. Mesh import is not differentiable.
- `rfx.interop` can emit a validated design document and a runnable openEMS script for representable simulations.
- `run(until_decay=..., radiated_flux_box=...)` can stop on radiated-energy flux, and MSL extraction now resolves automatic probe offsets against both documented clearances. Returned MSL S-matrices enforce passivity by default while retaining raw results in `S_raw`.

## v1.6.6 — June 2026
- Maintenance-only release: a behavior-preserving rectangular-waveguide mode-solver refactor and shipped-documentation hygiene. There was no public API or user-visible behavior change.
## v1.6.5 — June 2026

- [Validation reporting](/rfx/guide/validation/) is stricter: a promoted port-family result now requires magnitude evidence, a phase-consistency witness, an AD-vs-finite-difference check where gradients are part of the claim, and committed (reproducible) artifacts.
- Port-family support wording is clearer: users should match each source or port family to its documented calculator and evidence envelope instead of treating any S-parameter helper as universal.
- `compute_msl_s_matrix` now reports a positive characteristic impedance on both ports. This fixes reported metadata while leaving the S-parameter calculation invariant.
- Non-uniform mesh accuracy coverage now includes an analytic gated cavity case whose resonance frequency depends on the graded axis.
- Public guides and support pages were tightened around maintained workflows, validation boundaries, and device-count-adaptive test behavior.

## v1.6.4 — June 2026

- Rectangular waveguide-port evidence now covers WR-band empty-guide, PEC-short, and dielectric-slab cases with analytic and external-reference comparisons.
- `preflight()` and `preflight_sparameters()` now expose coded report objects for automation while preserving older list/string behavior.
- Run/forward results, S-matrix calculators, sweeps, and optimizers now surface finite-data, passivity, setup, and NaN-gradient issues earlier.
- `compute_coaxial_line_reflection(...)` is the current coaxial line-reflection path inside its documented transmission-line envelope.
- `compute_waveguide_s_matrix(checkpoint_segments=...)` adds segmented checkpointing for uniform waveguide AD-heavy runs.
- Finite-size flux monitors are regression-locked against the matching full-plane integrand window; the old finite-size instability caveat is superseded.
- The directivity objective gained a log-ratio option for sign-correct gradients when design variables change total radiated power.
