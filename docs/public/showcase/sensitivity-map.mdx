A 39.7 × 49.25 mm patch on 3.175 mm RT/Duroid 5880 (εr 2.2), fed by a 50 Ω
probe, resonates near 2.36 GHz. We ask how |S11| at f_t, 1 % above the
return-loss minimum, changes when the permittivity of any one substrate cell
changes. rfx answers for every cell under and around the patch with one
forward and one reverse pass: 20,592 cells on a mesh of 4 cells through the
substrate, 555,984 cells on a mesh of 12. All numbers on this page are from
rfx v2.0.0.

## What the map shows

The derivative is positive wherever it is significant: the cells with a
positive derivative carry 0.9998 of the summed magnitude on the 12-cell mesh.
It is concentrated in two bands, under and just outside the two radiating
edges; along the centre line between them it is about 540 times weaker (450
on the 4-cell mesh). That is what cavity perturbation
predicts for the TM010 mode: a local permittivity change shifts the resonance in
proportion to the local |E_z|², and E_z of this mode peaks at the radiating
edges and vanishes on the centre line. The map summed through the substrate and |E_z(f_t)|² from the same run
correlate at r = 0.966 on the 12-cell mesh (0.948 on the 4-cell mesh). The sign is the expected one: raising εr lowers
the resonance, which moves the return-loss minimum further below 2.356 GHz and
raises |S11| there.

## Three meshes

The map is solved on meshes of 4, 8 and 12 cells through the substrate. From
one mesh to the next the normalised map changes by 0.078 and then 0.036, and
the maps correlate at r = 0.995 and then 0.999, so the map converges. The
return-loss minimum moves by +0.75 % and then +0.23 %. The depth of the |S11|
dip does not converge with this board's probe: the wire probe has no radius,
so its series reactance grows with every refinement. This page therefore
quotes no |S11| level.

## How the gradient was checked

- Finite differences, on the 4-cell mesh: six blocks of cells chosen before
  the run (at the radiating edges, on the centre line, outside the patch). On
  the four large enough to judge, reverse mode and central differences agree
  to 2.4×10⁻⁶–1.9×10⁻⁵ relative, in float64.
- Simulation time, on the 12-cell mesh: repeating the gradient with a 1.5×
  longer simulation time changes the judged block sums by at most 7.6×10⁻⁵
  and the whole gradient by 2.7×10⁻⁴, relative.
- Settling: at every frequency read, on every mesh, the field left after the
  record adds at most 3.1×10⁻⁴ of the recorded value.
- The same board is solved by rfx and by openEMS in the repository's
  cross-solver test `tests/crossval/rt5880_patch/`: the resonance differs by
  0.097 % and the input resistance at resonance by 0.42 %; that test does not
  judge the dip depth.

<p data-rfx="note">This map is for this board; it is not a manufacturing tolerance or a statement about other boards.</p>

## What the code looks like

{/* rfx-showcase-objective:start */}

```python
...  # imports, board, corners, freqs, n_steps, checkpoint_segments, eps_bg: see the full script

def J(eps_box):
    res = board.sim.forward(
        design_box=corners, design_eps_override=eps_box,
        design_box_holds_ports=True, n_steps=n_steps, checkpoint=False,
        checkpoint_segments=checkpoint_segments,
        port_s11_freqs=freqs, skip_preflight=True)
    s = res.s_params[0, 0]
    return jnp.real(s[0]) ** 2 + jnp.imag(s[0]) ** 2

v, g = jax.jit(jax.value_and_grad(J))(eps_bg)
```
